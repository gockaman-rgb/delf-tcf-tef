TCF Canada in Detroit: the Alliance française, the fee and the 2027 dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated October 26, 2026 · 5 min read

Near Detroit, the TCF Canada is held by the Alliance française de Détroit — the French Institute of Michigan — at 30800 Telegraph Road in Bingham Farms, the only approved TCF center in Michigan. It costs $330, the lowest fee we have found in the United States, and is taken on computer. On October 26, 2026, its booking page showed every session full until May 13, 2027 — November 12, December 10, then one Thursday a month — and June 10, 2027 as the only open date. When a date is full, the Alliance asks you to write: it can sometimes add exam slots.

Key facts
  • 1 approved center in Michigan: the Alliance française de Détroit / French Institute of Michigan, 30800 Telegraph Rd, Suite 2925, Bingham Farms.

Booking the TCF Canada in Detroit

The Alliance sells its sessions on a TCF exam registration page (afdetroit.org/tcf-exams), linked from its TCF page: each date shows its price and either «Full» or «Add to cart», and you can also pay at the office. Choose the TCF Canada: «You CANNOT take the TCF IRN or TP for immigration purposes (it will NOT be accepted by IRRC)», the page warns. When a date is full, write to afd@afdetroit.org — «We are sometimes able to add exam slots». Cancellation more than ten business days before the test is refunded minus a $40 fee; after that, only a documented illness or emergency, sent within a month, can lead to a new date, if France Éducation international approves it.

If June is too late, compare the other U.S. centers on our USA page: on October 26, 2026, Pluma Academy in Denver ($435) listed sessions on most weekdays until March 2027, while San Francisco had only a waitlist. Chicago, the nearest big city, has no TCF Canada — only the TEF Canada, which IRCC also accepts.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Detroit?

At the Alliance française de Détroit (French Institute of Michigan), 30800 Telegraph Road, Suite 2925, in Bingham Farms — the only approved TCF center in Michigan. The test is on computer, except the speaking part.

When is the next TCF Canada in Detroit?

On October 26, 2026, every session until May 13, 2027 was full (November 12, December 10, then January 14, February 11, March 11, April 15 and May 13); June 10, 2027 was open. The Alliance sometimes adds slots: ask at afd@afdetroit.org.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Detroit?

$330 at the Alliance française de Détroit (October 26, 2026). A cancellation more than ten business days before the test is refunded minus $40.

Can I take the TCF IRN or tout public in Detroit for Canada?

No: the Alliance warns that the TCF IRN and the TCF tout public are not accepted by IRCC. For Express Entry or citizenship, book the TCF Canada.
