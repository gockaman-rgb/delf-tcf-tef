TCF Canada in Kuwait: what the Institut français offers

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

اقرأ بالعربية

Not as far as its website shows. France Éducation international lists one approved TCF centre in Kuwait — the Institut français du Koweït, in Jabriya — but on 26 October 2026 its exam pages offered the DELF, the DALF and the TEF, with no TCF Canada session or fee. IRCC accepts both tests, and the TEF Canada costs 130 KD there. To ask about the TCF Canada, contact the institute.

Key facts
  • 1 approved TCF centre in Kuwait (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Institut français, Jabriya (Hawalli); FEI shows computer-based sessions.
  • TCF Canada: no session or fee published on the institute’s website on 26 October 2026.
  • TEF Canada: 130 KD (listening, reading, speaking, writing); TEF 80 KD, TEFAQ 70 KD; 5 KD off for the institute’s students.
  • Registration: form, copy of the passport data page, passport photo and payment, at the institute.
  • IRCC accepts the TCF Canada and the TEF Canada alike; results are valid for two years.

The institute’s current exam pages (ifk.edu.kw) cover the DELF, the DALF and the TEF, which is run by the Paris Chamber of Commerce and Industry (Le français des affaires), not by France Éducation international. An older “Diploma & Tests” page describes TCF versions — the TCF DAP, the TCF ANF, the TCF Québec — and says the TCF can serve for “immigration to Canada”, but it names no TCF Canada and gives no date or fee. To register for the TEF, you fill in the form (at the institute or on its website), enclose a copy of your passport data page and a passport photo, and pay; students of the institute get 5 KD off. The website on FEI’s list, institutfrancais-koweit.com, no longer opens: the institute’s site is ifk.edu.kw.

If you need the TCF Canada itself

Contact the institute first (+965 6626 6980, cours@ifk.edu.kw): FEI’s approval allows it to hold the TCF, but its website announced no session. Otherwise, the nearest TCF Canada sessions we found are in Doha (1,300 QAR; the 16 December 2026 session was marked “registration closed” on 26 October), Saudi Arabia (1,950 SAR) and Dubai and Abu Dhabi (1,800 AED). Frequently asked questions

Can I take the TCF Canada in Kuwait?

FEI approves the Institut français du Koweït for the TCF, but on 26 October 2026 its website published no TCF Canada session or fee — only the TEF Canada, at 130 KD. Ask the institute directly.

How much does the TEF Canada cost in Kuwait?

130 KD for the four sections at the Institut français du Koweït (26 October 2026), with 5 KD off for the institute’s students.

Does IRCC accept the TEF Canada?

Yes: IRCC accepts two French tests, the TCF Canada and the TEF Canada, and converts either result into CLB levels; results are valid for two years.

Where is the nearest TCF Canada session?

In Doha at the Institut français du Qatar (1,300 QAR, 16 December 2026), in Saudi Arabia at the Alliance française d’Arabie Saoudite (1,950 SAR), and in Dubai and Abu Dhabi (1,800 AED).
