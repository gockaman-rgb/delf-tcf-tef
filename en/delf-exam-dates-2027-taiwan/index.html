DELF exam dates 2027 in Taiwan: the published sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 4 min read

Taiwan has published its DELF exam dates for 2027: B1 and B2 on 10 January in Taipei (registration 3–17 November 2026), then A1 to DALF C1 in May, the DALF C2 on 27 June, a Taichung session on 3–4 July and a November session. You book with the Alliance française de Taiwan — at its Taipei or Kaohsiung centre, or online: alliancefrancaise.org.tw.

Key facts
  • Next registration: 3–17 November 2026, for B1 and B2 on Sunday 10 January 2027 in Taipei (70 places for B1, 50 for B2).
  • May 2027, Taipei and Kaohsiung: written papers on 7 May (A1, A2, C1) and 14 May (B1, B2); registration 23 February – 9 March.
  • DALF C2: 27 June 2027. Taichung (Tunghai University): 3–4 July 2027. November 2027: 12 and 19 November.
  • Fees 2027: A1 NTD 3,500 · A2 4,000 · B1 4,600 · B2 5,000 · C1 6,200 · C2 6,700.
  • DELF junior: 18 and 25 July 2027, registration 11–25 May.
  • You may not sit the same level in November 2026 and January 2027.

The 2027 sessions

The Alliance française de Taiwan, the approved DELF-DALF centre, had its 2027 calendar online on 26 October 2026. Each level has a morning or afternoon written paper on a set day; the speaking test is on a separate day, sometimes before the written papers.

November 2026 and the fees before 2027

The November 2026 session, in Taipei and Kaohsiung, closed its registration on 14 September: A1 and A2 written papers on 20 November (speaking on 15 November), B1 and B2 on 27 November (speaking on 29 November and 6 December), DALF C1 on 20 November (speaking on 22 November). Its fees were the 2026 ones: A1 NTD 3,300, A2 3,800, B1 4,400, B2 4,800, C1 6,000.

How to register

  • In person: at the Taipei centre for the Taipei sessions, at the Kaohsiung centre for the Kaohsiung and Taichung sessions, with your ID; you fill in the form and pay there.
  • Online, Taipei: fill in the registration form, create an account, then pay with the payment link the Alliance sends you; your registration is valid once you receive the receipt.
  • Online, Kaohsiung: pay online first, then fill in the registration form straight away — a candidate who does not complete the form cannot sit the exam and is not refunded.

Adults, juniors and university sessions

The DELF-DALF tout public is open to candidates of all ages. Junior- and senior-high-school students can take the DELF junior instead: same structure, same value, topics chosen for teenagers, identical diploma — in July 2027. In June, the Alliance also runs private sessions for Fu Jen Catholic University and National Central University, reserved for their students, who register through their French department.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Taiwan as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Taiwan?

B1 and B2 on 10 January 2027 in Taipei; A1, A2 and C1 on 7 May and B1, B2 on 14 May in Taipei and Kaohsiung; the DALF C2 on 27 June; A1 to B2 on 3–4 July in Taichung; and A1, A2, C1 on 12 November, B1, B2 on 19 November 2027. The DELF junior is on 18 and 25 July 2027.

When can I register for the next DELF in Taiwan?

From 3 to 17 November 2026, for the B1 and B2 on 10 January 2027 in Taipei (70 places for B1, 50 for B2). The May 2027 session opens from 23 February to 9 March 2027.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Taiwan?

NTD 5,000 for the 2027 sessions (NTD 4,800 in November 2026). In 2027: A1 NTD 3,500, A2 4,000, B1 4,600, DALF C1 6,200 and C2 6,700.

Can adults take the DELF in Taiwan?

Yes: registration for the DELF-DALF tout public is open to candidates of all ages. Secondary-school students may take the DELF junior instead, which leads to the same diploma.

How do I book the DELF in Taiwan?

In person at the Alliance française de Taiwan in Taipei or Kaohsiung with your ID, or online: in Taipei, form first then payment link; in Kaohsiung, online payment first, then the form.
