TCF Canada in Switzerland: test centres, CHF fees and the next sessions

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Switzerland, 7 centres are approved for the TCF, but only two advertise the TCF Canada: ALPADIA Language Schools in Montreux (CHF 290, but no 2026 dates published) and the Alliance française de Zurich (dates and quotes by email). Swiss Exams — Bern, Geneva, Lausanne, Winterthur — and the University of Fribourg’s language centre only run the TCF tout public, on computer, at CHF 320 and CHF 300. Next session: Fribourg on 12 November 2026. For a Canadian application, the TEF Canada also had sessions published in Geneva on 26 October 2026.

Key facts
  • 7 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): Swiss Exams in four cities, the University of Fribourg, ALPADIA in Montreux and the Alliance française de Zurich.
  • TCF Canada: ALPADIA (Montreux), CHF 290, dates published for 2025 only; the Alliance française de Zurich, on request.
  • Computer-based TCF tout public: CHF 320 at Swiss Exams (CHF 160 for the three compulsory tests), CHF 300 in Fribourg (CHF 155).
  • Next tout public sessions: Fribourg 12 Nov 2026 (deadline 29 Oct), Lausanne 21 Nov (deadline 23 Oct), Geneva 4 Dec and Bern 5 Dec 2026 (deadline 6 Nov).
  • According to the University of Fribourg, the TCF tout public counts for French citizenship if all five tests are taken in the same session.
  • TEF Canada, the other test IRCC accepts: sessions published at IFAGE in Geneva on 26 October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Switzerland?

On 26 October 2026, two approved centres advertised it: ALPADIA Language Schools in Montreux (CHF 290, 2026 dates not published: call the school) and the Alliance française de Zurich (dates and quotes by email). Swiss Exams and the University of Fribourg only run the TCF tout public.

How much does the TCF cost in Switzerland?

CHF 320 for the full TCF tout public at Swiss Exams (CHF 160 for the three compulsory tests), CHF 300 at the University of Fribourg, CHF 290 for the TCF Canada at ALPADIA in Montreux.

When is the next TCF in Geneva or Lausanne?

At Swiss Exams: Lausanne on 21 November 2026 (deadline 23 October) and Geneva on 4 December 2026 (deadline 6 November), then Lausanne on 30 January and 27 February 2027 and Geneva on 6 February 2027 — TCF tout public, on computer.

Is the TCF tout public enough for French citizenship?

According to the University of Fribourg’s language centre, it counts for a citizenship application if the compulsory tests and both optional tests are taken in the same session. For a Canadian application, you need the TCF Canada or the TEF Canada.

Where can I take the TEF Canada in Switzerland?

The map of Le français des affaires showed TEF Canada sessions at IFAGE in Geneva on 26 October 2026. The TEF Canada is the other French test IRCC accepts.
