DELF exam dates 2027 in the Netherlands: sessions, fees and the 7 centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

DELF exam dates 2027 in the Netherlands (DELF examendata 2027): the Alliance française in Amsterdam announces two sessions, from 9 to 11 March (registration from January) and from 7 to 11 June 2027 (from April). The November 2026 session, 9 to 13 November, closed registration on 25 and 26 October. The DELF and DALF are held at 7 centres, including the Alliances françaises in Amsterdam, The Hague and Rotterdam and the Institut français in Groningen; the DELF B2 costs €175.

Key facts
  • 7 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), managed by the Institut français des Pays-Bas (The Hague): three Alliances françaises, the Institut français in Groningen and The Hague, the Lycée français Van Gogh and HAN in Arnhem.
  • 2027 in Amsterdam: 9–11 March and 7–11 June; registration opens in January and April 2027. The Hague announces sessions in March, June and November.
  • November 2026: A1 and A2 on the 9th, B1 on the 10th, B2 on the 11th, C1 on the 12th, C2 on 13 November; registration closed on 25 October in Amsterdam, on 26 October at the Institut français.
  • Fees (Amsterdam, Institut français): A1 €110 · A2 €110 · B1 €155 · B2 €175 · C1 €220 · C2 €230.
  • DELF junior in Amsterdam: A1 and A2 on 12 April, B1 and B2 on 13 April 2027 (B2 €180); DELF Prim on 26 May 2027.
  • Rotterdam runs exams “throughout the year”; dates on request.

Registration

Each centre handles its own registrations: in Amsterdam, they open in January for March and in April for June; in The Hague, one button per session (March, June, November) leads to the Alliance’s booking module; in Groningen, the Institut français answers by email. The diplomas are independent: you register directly for the level you want, and you need 50 points out of 100 to pass.

The 7 DELF-DALF test centres, city by city

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Institut français des Pays-Bas (The Hague) · Anna Paulownastraat 76, 2518 BJ La Haye · institutfrancais.nl. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in the Netherlands?

The Alliance française in Amsterdam announces two tout public sessions in 2027: 9 to 11 March (registration from January) and 7 to 11 June (from April). The Hague plans sessions in March, June and November; the level-by-level dates were not published on 26 October 2026.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in the Netherlands?

€175 at the Alliance française in Amsterdam and the Institut français des Pays-Bas; €110 for A1 or A2, €155 for B1, €220 for the DALF C1 and €230 for C2. The DELF junior B2 costs €180 in Amsterdam.

Can I still register for November 2026?

Not in Amsterdam, where registration closed on 25 October; the Institut français took registrations until 26 October 2026. The papers run from 9 to 13 November.

Where can I take the DELF in The Hague?

At the Alliance française in The Hague, which runs sessions in March, June and November. The Institut français des Pays-Bas and the Lycée français Van Gogh are also on FEI’s list.
