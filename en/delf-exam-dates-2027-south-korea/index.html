DELF exam dates 2027 in South Korea: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 4 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in South Korea had not been published on 26 October 2026. The last 2026 session is under way: B1 and B2 on 7–8 November, A1, A2 and the DALF on 21–22 November (registration closed on 14 October). You register online with Korea’s national DELF-DALF centre, delf-dalf.co.kr, run by the French Embassy’s language centre in Seoul. In 2026 it opened four adult sessions — March, May, September and November — each with a registration window of about two weeks.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026); they appear on the calendar page of delf-dalf.co.kr.
  • Next exams: B1 and B2 on 7–8 November 2026; A1, A2, DALF C1 and C2 on 21–22 November.
  • 4 adult sessions in 2026: March, May, September (A2, B1 and B2 only) and November.
  • Fees 2026: A1 150,000 won · A2 190,000 · B1 260,000 · B2 290,000 · C1 330,000 · C2 350,000.
  • 6 centres: Seoul, Busan, Daejeon, Daegu, Gwangju and Incheon.
  • Every candidate of secondary-school age sits the DELF junior; adults sit the tout public.

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

Korea has a single national calendar: the same exam days in every centre, set by the national DELF-DALF centre. Within a session, B1 and B2 come first, on a Saturday and Sunday; A1, A2 and the DALF follow two weeks later. The 2027 calendar had not been published on 26 October 2026; the 2026 one shows the pattern to expect.

How to register

Registration is online only, on the national centre’s platform: create an account on delf-dalf.co.kr (“S’inscrire”), choose the session, the level and the centre, and pay. The rules published for the November 2026 session:

  • a level can close before the deadline once it is full;
  • with fewer than five candidates for a level in a centre, that exam is cancelled and candidates may be moved to another centre;
  • you can cancel in “Mon espace” within seven days after registration closes, minus administrative fees; if you might cancel one level, register for each level separately, because a multi-level registration cannot be partly cancelled;
  • the notice to attend is printed from “Mon espace” on the Tuesday of the exam week.

Adults, juniors and children

Adults take the DELF-DALF tout public, with no upper age limit. In Korea, every candidate of secondary-school age is counted as a junior and sits the DELF junior — same levels, same diploma, topics chosen for teenagers. Primary-school children sit the DELF Prim (A1.1, A1, A2).

Results and diplomas

Results and the provisional pass certificate (attestation de réussite) are announced in the news section of delf-dalf.co.kr; the diploma itself, printed in France, comes months later — on 16 October 2026 the centre announced the collection of the diplomas from the May 2026 session.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Korea as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in South Korea?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026, the national DELF-DALF centre held four adult sessions — March, May, September and November — with B1 and B2 on one weekend and A1, A2 and the DALF two weeks later; the 2027 calendar will appear on delf-dalf.co.kr.

When is the next DELF exam in Korea?

B1 and B2 on 7–8 November 2026, A1, A2, DALF C1 and C2 on 21–22 November 2026. Registration for that session closed on 14 October 2026.

How much does the DELF cost in Korea?

In 2026: A1 150,000 won, A2 190,000, B1 260,000, B2 290,000, DALF C1 330,000 and C2 350,000 won for adults; the DELF junior costs the same, and the DELF Prim 90,000 to 171,000 won.

Can adults take the DELF in Korea?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public is for adults, with no upper age limit. Candidates of secondary-school age are counted as juniors and sit the DELF junior, which leads to the same diploma.

How do I book the DELF in Korea?

Online only, on delf-dalf.co.kr: create an account, choose the session, level and centre (Seoul, Busan, Daejeon, Daegu, Gwangju or Incheon) and pay during the registration window, which lasted about two weeks in 2026.
