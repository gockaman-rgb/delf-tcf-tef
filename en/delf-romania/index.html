DELF exam dates 2027 in Romania: sessions, registration and the 10 centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Romania (date examen DELF 2027) were not yet published on 26 October 2026. The next session is on 14 and 15 November 2026, with online registration open until 3 November and results on 17 December. The DELF and DALF are held at 10 centres — the Institut français branches in Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, Iași and Timișoara and six Alliances françaises —, under the central management of the Institut français de Roumanie; in 2026, Bucharest held four sessions (February, April, June, November).

Key facts
  • 10 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Instituts français in Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, Iași and Timișoara, and the Alliances françaises in Brașov, Constanța, Craiova, Pitești, Ploiești and Suceava.
  • November 2026: papers on 14 and 15 November, registration from 14 September to 3 November, results on 17 December (Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca, Iași).
  • Bucharest, 2026: 14–15 February, 4–5 April, 6–7 June and 14–15 November; Cluj-Napoca and Iași: 14–15 March, 6–7 June and 14–15 November.
  • In November 2026, the Institut in Cluj-Napoca also runs the exam in Oradea; in March, it went to Bistrița, Satu Mare, Sibiu and Târgu Mureș.
  • Fees not shown on the Instituts’ DELF-DALF pages on 26 October 2026: ask the office.
  • Diploma 4 to 5 months after the results are posted (Iași); online registration, with a tutorial.

The DELF junior has its own calendar: in Iași, 14–15 March, 23–24 May and 14–15 November 2026, with spring papers in Piatra-Neamț, Focșani and Galați. Timișoara and the six Alliances françaises on the list were not checked: their contact details are below.

Registration and diploma

You register online, on the website of your chosen Institut français branch, which publishes a tutorial; the DELF-DALF rules are attached to each page. The diplomas are independent: you register directly for the level you want, and you need 50 points out of 100 to pass.

In Bucharest, you collect the diploma from the registration office (courses and exams) with an ID; in Iași, it arrives 4 to 5 months after the results are posted. Fees are not on these pages: ask the registration office of your chosen branch.

The 10 DELF-DALF test centres, city by city

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Gestion Centrale DELF/DALF (Bucharest) · Bd. Dacia, 77, 020051 Bucarest · institutfrancais.ro. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

How to read this list. It reproduces the official list of test centres approved by France Éducation international (FEI), read on 26 October 2026: name, address, phone, generic email and website as FEI publishes them; where the listed website no longer works or has moved, we give the one we opened that day. An organisation that is not on this list is not approved.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Romania?

Not yet published: on 26 October 2026, the Instituts français in Romania only listed their 2026 sessions. Bucharest held four (February, April, June, November), Cluj-Napoca and Iași three (March, June, November).

When is the next DELF exam in Romania?

On 14 and 15 November 2026, at the Instituts français in Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca and Iași; online registration stays open until 3 November, and results are announced on 17 December 2026.

How much does the DELF cost in Romania?

The Instituts français did not show their fees on their DELF-DALF pages on 26 October 2026: ask the registration office of your chosen branch.

When do I get my DELF diploma?

Results for the November 2026 session are announced on 17 December; in Iași, the diploma arrives 4 to 5 months after the results are posted. In Bucharest, you collect it with an ID.
