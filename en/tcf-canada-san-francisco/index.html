TCF Canada in San Francisco: the Alliance française, the fee and the waitlist

By Augusto Grone · Updated October 26, 2026 · 5 min read

In San Francisco, the TCF Canada is held by the Alliance française de San Francisco, 1345 Bush Street — the only approved TCF center in Northern California — for $460, «Full exam only», on computer. On October 26, 2026, its TCF calendar listed the last TCF Canada session of the year, November 6, on waitlist, and no 2027 date; the TCF Québec had no date left in 2026. You need a valid passport to register, and 25 days between two TCF attempts.

Key facts
  • 1 approved center: the Alliance française de San Francisco, 1345 Bush Street; exams «in digital format» since October 1, 2022.
  • TCF Canada $460, «Full exam only».
  • ⚠️ October 26, 2026: November 6 on waitlist, like August 1, September 4 and September 25; no 2027 date listed.
  • TCF Québec: «Plus de date en 2026» — the Alliance points candidates to the TCF Canada.
  • Changes: up to 30 days before, a refund minus $60 or a free change; from 30 to 8 days, a change or credit minus $60, no refund; in the last 8 days, none.
  • 25 days between two TCF attempts, whatever the version; results in about two weeks.

Booking the TCF Canada in San Francisco

The Alliance's TCF Canada page links to its registration form: you choose the session, fill in the form and pay — the registration counts only once both are in. A valid passport is required «at the time of registration until after the date of the exam»; no other ID is accepted, and a lost or expired passport means a new date and the administrative fee. Within eight days of the test nothing can be changed, and the Alliance allows at most two reschedulings a calendar year. Results come by email about two weeks after the test.

With November 6 on waitlist and no 2027 date, look elsewhere in California: on October 18, 2026, the Alliance Française de Los Angeles ($505) listed November 20, December 18 and monthly dates to June 2027, while San Diego was sold out. Every U.S. center is on our USA page.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in San Francisco?

At the Alliance française de San Francisco, 1345 Bush Street, on computer; it is the only approved TCF center in Northern California.

When is the next TCF Canada in San Francisco?

On October 26, 2026, the only remaining date was November 6, on waitlist, and no 2027 session was listed. Los Angeles had dates every month to June 2027 (October 18).

How much does the TCF Canada cost in San Francisco?

$460 for the full exam (October 26, 2026). A refund, minus $60, is possible only up to 30 days before the test.

How long must I wait between two TCF attempts in San Francisco?

25 days, «regardless of the version concerned», according to the Alliance's TCF policies.
