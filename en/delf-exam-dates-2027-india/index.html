DELF exam dates 2027 in India: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in India had not been published on 26 October 2026. The next session is 7–11 December 2026 — A1 on the 7th through C1 and C2 on the 11th — with registration from 9 to 24 November 2026 and results on 19 January 2027. The written papers are on the same days across India; you register at your Alliance Française — the Alliance Française de Delhi’s 2026 calendar gives every window.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026).
  • Next session: 7–11 December 2026, registration 9–24 November, results 19 January 2027.
  • The DELF is a national exam: same written-paper dates in every centre; the speaking test within a week either side.
  • 2026 adult sessions: March, June, September and December; DELF junior in July and December.
  • Fees at the Alliance Française de Delhi (with 18% GST): A1, A2 ₹11,210 · B1, B2 ₹13,263 · C1, C2 ₹14,549.
  • 16 approved centres, from Delhi and Mumbai to Trivandrum.

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

In India the DELF is a national exam: the dates in the calendar are those of the written papers, the same in every centre, and each centre may schedule the speaking test up to a week before or after. The 2027 calendar had not been published on 26 October 2026; the 2026 one, from the Alliance Française de Delhi (version 6, updated on 21 January 2026), shows the pattern.

How to register

Register with your Alliance Française during the registration window — for December 2026, 9 to 24 November. Registrations are accepted strictly within the announced deadlines, and late or incomplete applications are not considered (Alliance Française de Delhi).

The 16 exam centres

France Éducation international’s list of 26 October 2026 gives sixteen approved centres in India: the Alliances Françaises of Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Bhopal, Chandigarh, Chennai (Madras), Delhi, Goa (Panjim), Hyderabad, Jaipur, Kolkata (Alliance française du Bengale), Lucknow, Mumbai (Bombay), Pondicherry, Pune and Trivandrum, and the Institut français en Inde in New Delhi, listed as the central body. Taking the TCF Canada instead? See TCF Canada in India.

Adults, juniors and children

Adults sit the DELF-DALF tout public, with no upper age limit; school pupils can sit the DELF junior (July and December in 2026) or the DELF Prim. The diploma is the same and valid for life.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in India as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in India?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026 the tout public sessions were in March, June, September and December, with the same written-paper dates across India; the 2027 calendar will come from the Alliances Françaises.

When is the next DELF exam in India?

7–11 December 2026: A1 on 7 December, A2 on the 8th, B1 on the 9th, B2 on the 10th, C1 and C2 on the 11th. Registration runs from 9 to 24 November 2026 and results are due on 19 January 2027.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in India?

₹13,263 including 18% GST for an external candidate at the Alliance Française de Delhi (2026 fees); ₹8,756 at its special rate. Each Alliance sets its own fees.

Can adults take the DELF in India?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public is for adults, with no upper age limit; the DELF junior and DELF Prim are for school pupils.

How do I book the DELF in India?

At your Alliance Française, during the registration window — for December 2026, 9 to 24 November. Late or incomplete applications are not accepted.
