TCF Canada in the Netherlands: test centres, fees and dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In the Netherlands, 7 centres are approved for the TCF, and the three Alliances françaises — Amsterdam, The Hague and Rotterdam — advertise the TCF Canada. Amsterdam runs it on computer for €290: its 25 November 2026 session was full on 26 October, and the next one is on 13 January 2027 (register before 29 December). The Hague sells its sessions through its online booking module; Rotterdam shows €75 to €300 depending on the version. The four other centres are higher-education institutions.

Key facts
  • 7 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): three Alliances françaises, the Institut français des Pays-Bas in Groningen and three universities of applied sciences (Leeuwarden, Nijmegen, Utrecht).
  • TCF Canada in Amsterdam: €290; ⚠️ 25 Nov 2026 full; 13 Jan 2027 open, register before 29 Dec 2026.
  • TCF IRN in Amsterdam: €205, on 16 Dec 2026 (register before 1 Dec); TCF Québec: €75 per test, dates on request.
  • The Hague: TCF Canada, Québec, IRN and tout public, sessions booked online; results certificate within 15 working days.
  • Rotterdam: TCF tout public, Québec, Canada and IRN, €75 to €300 depending on the version.
  • TEF Canada, the other test IRCC accepts: sessions published by the Alliance française in The Hague on 26 October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in the Netherlands?

At one of the three Alliances françaises, which advertised it on 26 October 2026: Amsterdam (€290, on computer), The Hague and Rotterdam.

When is the next TCF Canada in Amsterdam?

The 25 November 2026 session was full on 26 October; the next one is on 13 January 2027, with registration before 29 December 2026.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in the Netherlands?

€290 at the Alliance française in Amsterdam. Rotterdam shows €75 to €300 depending on the version; The Hague sells its sessions through its online module.

Where can I take the TCF IRN for French citizenship in the Netherlands?

At the Alliance française in Amsterdam, €205, on 16 December 2026 (register before 1 December), or at the Alliances in The Hague and Rotterdam.
