TCF Canada in Houston: the Alliance Française, the fee, the dates — and the TEF Canada

By Augusto Grone · Updated October 26, 2026 · 5 min read

In Houston, the TCF Canada is held by the Alliance Française de Houston, 427 Lovett Boulevard, the only approved TCF center in Texas. On October 8, 2026, it was selling a session every Wednesday, ten seats each, for $400, through January 27, 2027. On October 26, its TCF page listed no session and no fee — only the address exams@alliancehouston.org — while its TEF Canada, the other French test IRCC accepts, had seats twice a week for $380, from October 30, 2026 to January 18, 2027.

TCF or TEF Canada in Houston

Until mid-October, the Alliance published its TCF sessions on its TCF page — one every Wednesday, ten seats each, registration closing three days before, $400. On October 26, 2026, that page described the four TCF versions (tout public, Canada, Québec, IRN) but listed no date and no fee: write to exams@alliancehouston.org, or call 713-526-1121, to ask when TCF Canada sessions resume. Whatever you book, «Exam registrations are non-refundable for any reason».

The Alliance's TEF Canada page, on the other hand, was selling sessions: $380, on Mondays and Fridays, from October 30 to December 7, 2026 and from January 8 to 18, 2027, with 1 to 10 seats left. IRCC accepts the TEF Canada like the TCF Canada, with its own conversion to CLB levels (TCF vs TEF Canada). For the TCF Canada itself, the other U.S. centers and their fees are on our USA page; Pluma Academy in Denver listed sessions on most weekdays until March 2027.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Houston?

At the Alliance Française de Houston, 427 Lovett Boulevard — the only approved TCF center in Texas.

When is the next TCF Canada in Houston?

On October 26, 2026, the Alliance's TCF page listed no session. On October 8 it was selling one every Wednesday through January 27, 2027; ask exams@alliancehouston.org whether those dates still stand.
