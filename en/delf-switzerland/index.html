DELF exam dates 2027 in Switzerland: the national calendar, fees and the 12 centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

DELF exam dates 2027 in Switzerland: the DELF and DALF tout public follow a national calendar, published on delfdalf.ch by the Fondation Esprit Francophonie. In 2027 the written papers are on Saturday 20 March (registration until 8 January), then 22 May, 19 June, 18 September and 13 November. You register online with one of the regional centres, where the DELF B2 costs CHF 370 to 390. The 14 November 2026 session has been closed since 11 September.

Key facts
  • 12 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), managed by the cooperation and cultural action service named on FEI’s list (Zurich); calendar and registration on delfdalf.ch.
  • 2027: 20 March (deadline 8 January), 22 May (12 March), 19 June (9 April), 18 September (6 August), 13 November (10 September).
  • Speaking tests during the session month, depending on the centre; DALF C2 speaking test the day before the written papers.
  • DELF B2: CHF 370 in Neuchâtel, 375 in Geneva and Lausanne, 380 in Bern and Liestal, 390 in Zurich (KV Zürich).
  • September: four centres only (Geneva, Liestal, Lugano, St. Gallen); DELF junior in March, May, June and November; DELF Prim in May and June.
  • Exam programme sent by email at least two weeks before the first paper.

The March and November sessions bring together the most centres — Aarau, Bellinzona, Bern, Geneva, Lausanne, Liestal, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, St. Gallen, Sierre, Weinfelden and the two Zurich centres; the September session is held only in Geneva, Liestal, Lugano and St. Gallen. The 2026 calendar put the last session of the year on Saturday 14 November 2026, with a deadline of 11 September.

Registration

You register online from delfdalf.ch, choosing your regional centre. Your personal exam programme — with the addresses of the papers, which are not always held in the same building — arrives by email at least two weeks before the first paper; the programme and the results are only sent electronically, hence the advice to give a private email address.

The 12 approved DELF-DALF test centres in Switzerland

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Service de coopération et d'action culturelle (Zurich) · Kappelergasse 15, 8001 Zurich · delfdalf.ch. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Switzerland?

The written papers are on Saturday 20 March, 22 May, 19 June, 18 September and 13 November 2027; the registration deadlines are 8 January, 12 March, 9 April, 6 August and 10 September 2027.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Switzerland?

CHF 370 to CHF 390 depending on the centre: CHF 370 in Neuchâtel, 375 in Geneva and Lausanne, 380 in Bern and Liestal, 390 at KV Zürich. The DALF C1 costs CHF 410 to 420.

Can I still register for November 2026?

No: registration for the 14 November 2026 session closed on 11 September. The next session is on 20 March 2027, with a deadline of 8 January 2027.

Where can I take the DELF in Geneva or Lausanne?

At the Lac Léman exam centre, run by IFAGE (place des Augustins 19, Geneva), which also organises the Lausanne sessions: DELF B2 at CHF 375.

When do I get my exam programme?

By email, at least two weeks before the first paper. It gives the addresses of the papers, which sometimes change from one session to the next.
