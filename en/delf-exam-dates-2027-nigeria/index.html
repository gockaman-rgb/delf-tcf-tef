DELF exam dates 2027 in Nigeria: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 4 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Nigeria were not yet published on 26 October 2026 — but the December 2026 session is open: exams on 8–11 December at the Alliance Française de Lagos, with registration until 27 November. Lagos is home to Nigeria’s DELF-DALF exam office and held four adult sessions in 2026 — March, July, September and December. You register online with the Alliance: afnigeria.org/lagos.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026).
  • December 2026, Lagos: exams 8–11 December; registration 1 October – 27 November.
  • 4 adult sessions in Lagos in 2026: 10–13 March, 21–24 July, 21–24 September and 8–11 December.
  • Fees in Lagos: A1, A2 ₦35,000 · B1, B2 ₦80,000 · C1, C2 ₦150,000; 10% less for AF Lagos students.
  • Port Harcourt: two sessions in 2026, 10–13 March and 21–24 July, at the same fees.
  • 10 approved centres: Lagos, Abuja, Enugu, Ilorin, Jos, Kaduna, Kano, Maiduguri, Owerri and Port Harcourt.

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

France Éducation international’s list of approved centres (26 October 2026) names the exam office of the Alliance Française de Lagos as Nigeria’s DELF-DALF body. Its 2026 calendar had four adult sessions, each over four days; the Alliance warns that the calendar “might be subject to changes in case of exceptional circumstances”. The 2027 dates had not been published on 26 October 2026.

Other Alliances set their own sessions. The Alliance Française de Port Harcourt published two for 2026: A1 on 10 March, A2 on 11, B1 on 12, B2 on 13 and C1 on 10 March (registration deadline 20 February), then the same levels on 21–24 July (deadline 3 July); no later 2026 session was listed there.

Juniors and children have their own sessions in Lagos: DELF Junior on 3–5 June, 1–3 July and 24–26 November 2026 (registration 1 October – 13 November), DELF Prim on 20–22 May and 24–26 June.

How to register

Anyone can take the DELF-DALF at the Alliance Française de Lagos, not only its students. Register online through the link on its DELF/DALF page, then wait for the confirmation e-mail, which comes during the week after your registration; questions go to the Lagos exam office (certification.lagos@afnigeria.org). The Alliance stresses that its registration process and deadlines are compulsory, and the exam regulations are sent when you register. In Port Harcourt, register with the Alliance Française de Port Harcourt (af-nigeria.org) before its deadline.

Adults, juniors and children

Adults sit the DELF-DALF tout public. In Lagos the DELF Junior is for secondary-school pupils aged 12 to 17 and the DELF Prim for primary pupils aged 7 to 11; the diplomas have the same value and are valid for life.

Results and diplomas

The Alliance Française de Lagos publishes the results as PDF lists on its DELF/DALF page (the September 2026 results were online on 26 October). It says you usually receive a certificate about a month after the exam and the diploma three months later; in Lagos the provisional certificate (attestation) is issued on request at the exam office of the Ikoyi centre, on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 9 am to 3 pm.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Nigeria as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Nigeria?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026 the Alliance Française de Lagos held adult sessions on 10–13 March, 21–24 July, 21–24 September and 8–11 December; Port Harcourt held two, in March and July.

When is the next DELF exam in Nigeria?

8–11 December 2026 at the Alliance Française de Lagos, with registration open from 1 October to 27 November 2026. The DELF Junior is on 24–26 November 2026 (registration until 13 November).

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Nigeria?

₦80,000 at the Alliance Française de Lagos in 2026, or ₦72,000 for its students. A1 and A2 cost ₦35,000, C1 and C2 ₦150,000; Port Harcourt lists the same adult fees.

Can adults take the DELF in Nigeria?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public is the adult version, open to everyone, not only Alliance students. Pupils can take the DELF Junior or the DELF Prim.

How do I book the DELF in Nigeria?

Online through the Alliance Française de Lagos’s DELF/DALF page during the registration window — for December 2026, until 27 November — then wait for the confirmation e-mail. In other cities, register with the local Alliance Française or the Institut français in Abuja.
