DELF exam dates 2027 in Greece: the December and May sessions, fees and registration

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

DELF exam dates 2027 in Greece (εξετάσεις DELF 2027): the DELF B1 and B2 are on Saturday 15 May 2027, the A1, A2 and DALF on Friday 14 May, with online registration from 1 to 15 February 2027. Before that, the December session is on 4 and 5 December 2026 (registration closed on 11 October). The Institut français de Grèce, the only centre on FEI’s list, runs both sessions in Athens, Thessaloniki and regional centres; the DELF B1 and B2 cost €160.

Key facts
  • 1 approved centre (FEI list of 26 October 2026), the Institut français de Grèce, which sets each session’s exam centres according to the number of candidates per region.
  • May 2027: A1, A2, C1 and C2 on Friday 14 May; B1 and B2 on Saturday 15 May; registration from 1 to 15 February 2027, online only.
  • December 2026: A1, A2, C1 and C2 on 4 December, B1 and B2 on 5 December; registration closed on 11 October.
  • 2026 fees: A1 and A2 €75 · B1 and B2 €160 · C1 €180 · C2 €200.
  • Speaking tests in Athens and Thessaloniki in the days after the written papers; in regional centres, on the written-paper weekend.
  • The programme states that no request to change a written or speaking test is accepted.

Registration: 1 to 11 October 2026 for December (closed), 1 to 15 February 2027 for May. You cannot register for two diplomas whose times overlap: A1 and C1, for example, both start on Friday at 17:00. The DELF Prim A1 and A2, for under-12s, follow the A1 and A2 times. The Institut also runs the Sorbonne exams at the same sessions.

You pay when registering, online by card or at a bank with the payment code the platform issues. These are the fees shown for 2026; those for the May 2027 session were not published yet.

Registration and test day

Registration is online only, on the eservices.ifg.gr platform, which issues a payment code; it assumes you have read the exam programme and rules. Each session’s exam centres — Athens, Thessaloniki and regional towns — are only announced when registration closes, according to the number of candidates per region. You print the test-day notice, with your speaking-test time, from the Institut’s website.

The exams office of the Institut français de Grèce is at 31 Sina Street, Athens; the Institut français in Thessaloniki also advises candidates in the north. The diplomas are independent: you register directly for the level you want, and you need 50 points out of 100 to pass.

The approved DELF-DALF test centre in Greece

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Ambassade de France en Grèce - Institut français de Grèce (Athens) · 31, rue Sina, 106 80 Athènes · ifg.gr. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Greece?

For the May 2027 session: DELF A1, A2 and DALF C1, C2 on Friday 14 May, DELF B1 and B2 on Saturday 15 May 2027, with registration from 1 to 15 February 2027. The December 2027 session was not published on 26 October 2026.

When is the DELF B2 in December 2026?

On Saturday 5 December 2026, 18:30 to 21:00; the speaking test is on 10 December in Athens and Thessaloniki, on 6 December in regional centres. Registration closed on 11 October.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Greece?

€160, like B1, according to the Institut français de Grèce’s 2026 fees; €75 for A1 or A2, €180 for the DALF C1 and €200 for C2.

Where is the DELF held in Greece?

In Athens, Thessaloniki or a regional centre: the Institut français de Grèce, the country’s only approved centre, sets each session’s list of centres when registration closes, according to the number of candidates per region.

Do I need A1 before B2?

No: the DELF and DALF diplomas are independent, so you register directly for the level you want. The only rule in Greece: don’t pick two diplomas whose times overlap.
