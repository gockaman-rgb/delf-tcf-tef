DELF exam dates 2027 in Lebanon: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Lebanon had not been published on 26 October 2026. The next adult session is in November 2026 — B1 on 24 November, B2 on the 25th, C1 on the 26th, C2 on the 27th — and registration is open until 30 October at the Institut français du Liban, in Beirut and eight other towns. Calendar and fees: institutfrancais-liban.com.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026).
  • November 2026: B1 24 Nov · B2 25 · C1 26 · C2 27; registration 28 September – 30 October.
  • 5 adult sessions in 2026: January, April, June, September and November; junior in March, May and October.
  • Fees: A1 and A2 90 € · B1 120 € · B2 170 € · C1 and C2 210 €.
  • 9 centres of the Institut français du Liban: Beirut, Baalbek, Deir el Qamar, Jounieh, Nabatieh, Saida, Tripoli, Tyre, Zahlé.

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

The Institut français du Liban publishes one calendar for the whole country. Each level has its own written-paper day, and the individual speaking test follows over the next few days (periods in the table). The 2027 calendar had not been published on 26 October 2026. In 2026 the year opened with a January session for B1 to C2, whose registration ran from 10 November to 12 December 2025; whether 2027 will follow the same pattern was not announced.

The nine exam centres

France Éducation international’s list of 26 October 2026 gives nine centres, all branches of the Institut français du Liban: Beirut (the central body), Baalbek, Deir el Qamar, Jounieh, Nabatieh, Saida, Tripoli, Tyre and Zahlé. Preparing for Canada instead? See TCF Canada in Lebanon.

Adults, juniors and children

Adults sit the DELF-DALF tout public (sessions in January, April, June, September and November in 2026). Secondary-school pupils have their own DELF junior sessions (March, May and October) and primary pupils the DELF Prim (March). The diploma is the same and valid for life; the Institut français du Liban presents it as the key to higher education in Lebanon and in French-speaking countries.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Lebanon as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Lebanon?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026 the Institut français du Liban held tout public sessions in January, April, June, September and November; the January 2026 session had its registration from 10 November to 12 December 2025.

When is the next DELF exam in Lebanon?

B1 on 24 November 2026, B2 on 25 November, C1 on 26 November and C2 on 27 November, with the speaking test between 24 and 30 November. Registration is open from 28 September to 30 October 2026.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Lebanon?

170 € (2026 calendar of the Institut français du Liban). A1 and A2 cost 90 €, B1 120 €, DALF C1 and C2 210 €.

Can adults take the DELF in Lebanon?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public is the adult version, with five sessions in 2026. Pupils have separate DELF junior and DELF Prim sessions.

How do I book the DELF in Lebanon?

At the Institut français du Liban branch where you want to sit the exam — Beirut, Baalbek, Deir el Qamar, Jounieh, Nabatieh, Saida, Tripoli, Tyre or Zahlé — during the registration window.
