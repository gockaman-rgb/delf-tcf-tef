DELF and DALF exam dates 2026–2027: France’s session calendar and registration windows

By Augusto Grone · Updated 17 October 2026 · 6 min read

Lire en français · Leer en español

In France, the DELF and DALF tout public — the general-public versions — are held at ten national sessions a year, set by France Éducation international: one a month, never in April or September. The last three sessions of 2026 have their written papers on 6–8 October, 3–5 November and 1–3 December; 2027 starts on 12–14 January. But each centre opens only some of these sessions and closes registration four to ten weeks before the written papers — sometimes over just two days. These are France’s dates: other countries set their own, and we give the UK, the US and the UAE below. The full calendar, the registration windows we found, and how not to miss yours.

Key facts
  • 10 national sessions a year in France: January, February, March, May, June, July, August, October, November, December.
  • 2026: 6–8 October, 3–5 November, 1–3 December. 2027: 12–14 Jan, 2–4 Feb, 16–18 Mar, 25–27 May, 15–17 Jun, 6–8 Jul, 3–5 Aug, 5–7 Oct, 16–18 Nov, 7–9 Dec.
  • Each centre chooses its sessions: Nantes Université opens four a year, the Alliance française de Lyon all ten.
  • Registration closes 4 to 10 weeks before: until 4 October for November at the Alliance française de Paris, just two days (22–23 September) for December in Nantes.
  • Results within 4 to 6 weeks; the diploma is valid for life.
  • ⚠️ This is France’s calendar. Outside France, each country sets its own dates — the UK, the US and the UAE.

France’s national DELF-DALF calendar, 2026–2027

Every year, France Éducation international publishes the calendar of the DELF-DALF tout public sessions in France (the Prim, junior and school versions have their own dates). The dates below are those of the group written papers; the individual speaking test is held around the written papers, on a notice from the centre — up to two weeks later in Lille or Bordeaux.

Within a session, each level has its own day: at the DEFLE in Bordeaux, in December 2026, A1 and A2 candidates sit on the 1st, B1 and B2 on the 2nd, the DALF on the 3rd. The DALF C1 and every DELF level, from A1 to B2, therefore share these ten sessions, each on its own day.

This calendar is France’s. Outside France, the dates are set country by country, and they don’t necessarily match: in the UK, for example, the December 2026 written papers run from 2 to 14 December depending on the level. See DELF dates outside France.

Registration windows

This is the real constraint of the calendar. A DELF centre orders its exam papers from FEI several weeks before the written papers, so registration closes four to ten weeks before, and some centres open only a short window — a week in Lille, three weeks at the Sorbonne Nouvelle, two days in Nantes, “subject to available places”. At others, the window is long but the session fills up: at the Alliance française de Lyon, October and November 2026 were already full on 17 September.

Documents and payment are the same everywhere: a valid ID, your candidate number if you have already taken a DELF, payment by card, and non-refundable fees. One more rule at the Alliance française Aix-Marseille: “you cannot re-register for the DELF/DALF at the same level until the results of the previous session have been announced”.

Choosing your session

Count backwards from the date by which you need the diploma. Results arrive within four to six weeks (six at the Alliance française de Paris and in Lyon), and with them the provisional pass certificate (attestation de réussite), which is enough for most authorities; the diploma itself comes later. So a December 2026 session gives you a result in January 2027: for a French citizenship or resident-card application to be filed in the spring, it is the last stress-free one.

If the date is too close to register — or if your centre’s sessions are full — there are two ways out: another approved centre in the region, which won’t necessarily have the same sessions or the same fees (from €125 to €280 for a B2: see DELF B2 dates and fees), or a test — the TCF IRN is held almost every week, but its results certificate is valid for two years, whereas the DELF is valid for life (diploma or test: which to choose). The level pages explain each exam: DELF B1, DELF B2, DALF C1 and C2; our guide to where to take the DELF in France, Où passer le DELF en France ?, is in French.

DELF dates outside France

France’s calendar doesn’t apply abroad: each country sets its own session dates, and you register with a centre there. Three of our country pages, in English, give the next dates:

  • United Kingdom: the written papers fall on the same days at every centre — December 2026 (B1 on the 2nd, B2 on the 3rd, C1 on the 9th; registration in London from 15 to 29 October), then March 2027 (B1 on 10 March, B2 on 15 March, C1 on 18 March) and June 2027 (B1 on 2 June, B2 on 7 June, C1 on 10 June), as the centres’ websites showed on 8 October 2026. DELF in the UK.
  • United States: the next tout public session runs from 7 to 11 December 2026 — A1 and A2 on Monday, B1 on Tuesday, B2 on Wednesday, C1 on Thursday, C2 on Friday — with registration closing between 7 November (Houston) and 30 November (Chicago); no 2027 dates had been published on 8 October 2026. DELF in the USA.
  • United Arab Emirates: the Alliance Française de Dubai’s next adult session is on Friday 11 December 2026, as published on 13 October 2026; no 2027 session had been published that day. DELF in Dubai and the UAE.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates for autumn 2026?

In France, the written papers of 6–8 October, 3–5 November and 1–3 December 2026, according to France Éducation international’s national calendar — each centre opens only some of these sessions, and its registration closes four to ten weeks before. Other countries set their own dates.

What are the DELF exam dates in 2027?

In France: 12–14 January, 2–4 February, 16–18 March, 25–27 May, 15–17 June, 6–8 July, 3–5 August, 5–7 October, 16–18 November and 7–9 December 2027 (written papers), according to FEI’s 2027 calendar checked on 17 September 2026. Each centre opens only some of these sessions.

When are the DALF C1 and DELF B1 exam dates in 2027?

In France, the DALF and every DELF level share the same ten national sessions, so the 2027 dates are the ones above; within a session, each level has its own day (at the DEFLE in Bordeaux in December 2026: B1 and B2 on the 2nd, the DALF on the 3rd). Abroad, the dates differ: in the UK, for example, the B1 falls on 10 March and 2 June 2027, the C1 on 18 March and 10 June 2027.

Why is there no DELF in April or September in France?

FEI’s national calendar has no tout public session in those two months, in 2026 as in 2027: ten sessions a year, one a month the rest of the year.

When should I register for the DELF?

As soon as your centre opens registration: four to ten weeks before the written papers, with windows that are sometimes very short — two days at Nantes Université, a week at the Alliance française de Lille — and sessions full two months ahead in Lyon.

Are DELF dates the same outside France?

Not necessarily: the calendar on this page is France’s, and each country sets its own dates. In the UK, the December 2026 written papers run from 2 to 14 December depending on the level; in the US, the next session is from 7 to 11 December 2026; in Dubai, on 11 December 2026. The exact date and registration window are always the centre’s: check its website.
