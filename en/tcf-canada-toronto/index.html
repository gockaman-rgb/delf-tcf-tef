Taking the TCF Canada in Toronto: the approved centres, the sessions and registration

By Augusto Grone · Updated 8 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Toronto, you can take the TCF Canada at 5 centres approved by France Éducation international: the four campuses of the Alliance française de Toronto — Spadina, North York, Mississauga, Oakville — and GB Language Centre in North York, all computer-based (paper is also possible at the Alliance). The Alliance’s sessions are quarterly, registration opens a month before at 10 am, and “If a session is not listed, it is full.” Below: the centres, their contacts, and how to get a seat.

Key facts
  • 5 approved centres in the area: Alliance française de Toronto (Spadina, North York, Mississauga, Oakville) and GB Language Centre.
  • Quarterly sessions at the Alliance; registration opens one month before, at 10 am.
  • Registration openings for 2027: 1 December 2026 (January to March), 2 March, 20 May, 17 August 2027.
  • “If a session is not listed, it is full”; no waitlist; one registration at a time.
  • FEI has dropped re-marking for sessions held since 1 September 2026.

How to book the TCF Canada in Toronto

In Canada, each centre handles its own registrations online, on its own website, with the passport you’ll use for your IRCC application. Registration is final, and cancelling costs money: the Alliance Française de Montréal keeps $75 if you cancel more than 15 days ahead, and refunds nothing after that. In the big cities, sessions fill up within minutes: create your account in advance and log in the moment registration opens. Allow at least 20 days between two attempts, whichever centre you test at. Results arrive by email within 2 to 4 weeks and are valid for two years. On test day, bring a valid passport and your printed test-day notice (admission letter).

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Toronto?

At the Alliance française de Toronto, on four campuses — Spadina (downtown), North York, Mississauga, Oakville — or at GB Language Centre in North York. Registration is online only.

When does TCF Canada registration open in Toronto?

A month before each quarter, at 10 am: for 2027, on 1 December 2026 (sessions from January to March), then on 2 March, 20 May and 17 August 2027. Seats go within minutes; there is no waitlist, so check back regularly — seats do free up.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Toronto?

The Alliance française de Toronto doesn’t post a fee on its TCF page; the price appears when you book. For reference: $390 in Vancouver, $400 in Edmonton, $440 at UQTR.

Paper or computer-based in Toronto?

The Alliance française offers both formats; GB Language Centre lists computer-based sessions.
