TCF Canada in Ireland: Dublin and Galway, fees and registration

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Ireland, two centres are approved for the TCF, and both show the TCF Canada at €270: the Alliance française de Dublin, which holds TEF and TCF sessions every month on its computers, and The French Institute in Galway. In Dublin, you register by email with a copy of your passport, no later than one month before the exam; results arrive in two to three weeks. In Galway, dates are given on request.

Key facts
  • 2 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Alliance française de Dublin and The French Institute (Galway).
  • TCF Canada: €270 at both; in Dublin, TCF IRN €270, TCF Québec €270 (all four tests), TCF tout public €300.
  • Dublin: sessions “every month”; registration closes one month before each exam; the test is taken on the centre’s computers.
  • Results in 2 to 3 weeks in Dublin; results certificate valid for 2 years.
  • Galway: dates on request.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Ireland?

At the Alliance française de Dublin, 1 Kildare Street, or at The French Institute in Galway: both approved centres show the TCF Canada at €270.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Ireland?

€270 in Dublin and in Galway, as checked on 26 October 2026. In Dublin, the TCF IRN also costs €270 and the full TCF tout public €300.

When is the next TCF Canada in Dublin?

The Alliance française de Dublin holds TEF and TCF sessions every month; registration closes one month before each exam. You book by email with the exams office.

When do the results arrive?

Two to three weeks after the exam in Dublin; the results certificate is valid for two years.
