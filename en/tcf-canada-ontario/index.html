TCF Canada test centres in Ontario: where to book, the fees and the next dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Ontario has 8 approved TCF centres on France Éducation international's list of 26 October 2026: the four sites of the Alliance française de Toronto (Spadina, North York, Mississauga, Oakville), GB Language Centre in North York, the Alliance Française Ottawa, FrancoLangues in Kanata and Collège Boréal in Windsor. On 26 October, the TCF Canada cost $390 at the Alliance in Toronto, $400 in Ottawa and $452 at GB Language Centre; Ottawa had seats on 10 November, and Toronto opens its January–March 2027 sessions on 1 December at 10 am.

Key facts
  • 8 approved centres (FEI, 26 October 2026): Toronto (Spadina), North York (2), Mississauga, Oakville, Ottawa (2, including FrancoLangues in Kanata) and Windsor (Collège Boréal, paper-based, new on the list since 19 September).
  • Fees on 26 October 2026: $390 Alliance française de Toronto · $400 Alliance Française Ottawa · $452 GB Language Centre · FrancoLangues: given when you book.
  • Toronto: registration for January–March 2027 opens on 1 December 2026 at 10 am, then on 2 March, 20 May and 17 August 2027; «If a session is not listed, it is full»; no waitlist.
  • Ottawa: 10 November 2026, 6 and 8 January 2027 open on computer, 14 January 2027 on paper; further January sessions go on sale on 1 November 2026.
  • 20 days between two attempts at the Alliance in Toronto; 30 days at FrancoLangues.

How to book the TCF Canada in Ontario

In Toronto, the Alliance française sells its sessions only online, through an account you create beforehand: registration opens one month before each quarter at 10 am, and «If a session is not listed, it is full». Have your account and passport ready on 1 December 2026 at 10 am for the January–March 2027 sessions. «There is no waiting list», you can hold only one registration at a time across its four sites, and refunds are «not possible under any circumstances» — a transfer costs $100. GB Language Centre, in North York, sells the TCF Canada at $452 on its own website.

In Ottawa, the Alliance Française lists each session with an «Add to cart» button or the date its sales open; on 26 October, 10 November 2026 and 6 and 8 January 2027 were open on computer and 14 January 2027 on paper. FrancoLangues, at 105 Schneider Road in Kanata, takes bookings through its online appointment page and gives its fee when you book; it refunds nothing and changes no dates. In Windsor, Collège Boréal appears on FEI's list, but we found no TCF page: ask the college. Outside Ontario, the nearest open dates were in New Brunswick ($390).

The TEF Canada, which IRCC also accepts, is another way in: on 26 October 2026, the map of TEF centres of Le français des affaires (CCI Paris Île-de-France) showed TEF Canada sessions published at GB Language Centre and the Montreal Language Institute in North York, Collège Boréal in Toronto and Windsor, and Algonquin College in Ottawa (TCF vs TEF Canada).

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Ontario?

In the Toronto area, at the Alliance française de Toronto (Spadina, North York, Mississauga, Oakville) or GB Language Centre (North York); in Ottawa, at the Alliance Française (352 MacLaren Street) or FrancoLangues (Kanata); and Collège Boréal in Windsor is on FEI's list.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Ontario?

On 26 October 2026: $390 at the Alliance française de Toronto, $400 at the Alliance Française Ottawa, $452 at GB Language Centre; FrancoLangues gives its fee when you book.

When can I book the TCF Canada in Toronto?

Registration for January–March 2027 opens on 1 December 2026 at 10 am, then on 2 March, 20 May and 17 August 2027. Sessions go within minutes, and there is no waitlist.

Where are TCF Canada seats available in Ontario?

On 26 October 2026, at the Alliance Française Ottawa: 10 November 2026 and 6 and 8 January 2027 on computer, 14 January 2027 on paper, at $400.

Can I take the TCF Canada in North York?

Yes: at the Alliance française de Toronto's North York campus (47 Sheppard Avenue East), $390, or at GB Language Centre (716 Gordon Baker Road), $452.
