DELF exam dates 2027 in Madagascar: sessions and exam centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Madagascar were not published online on 26 October 2026 for the adult DELF and DALF. The Alliance française d’Antananarivo announces its next DELF Prim session for April 2027; its 2026 DELF junior was held on 5–8 October. For the tout public sessions, ask the Alliance française where you want to sit the exam — 24 of them are approved centres. Antananarivo’s catalogue: afantananarivo.mg.

Key facts
  • 2027: adult (tout public) dates not published online (checked on 26 October 2026).
  • DELF Prim (7–12): next session announced for April 2027 at the Alliance française d’Antananarivo.
  • DELF junior 2026 (13–17): exams on 5–8 October, registration 6 July – 12 September (Antananarivo).
  • 24 approved centres, all Alliances françaises, from Antsiranana and Nosy Be to Fort-Dauphin and Tuléar.
  • Exam fees for the adult DELF and DALF in ariary: not published online on 26 October 2026.
  • The national body on France Éducation international’s list: the French Embassy’s cultural cooperation service in Antananarivo.

What was published on 26 October 2026

Madagascar’s DELF-DALF centres are its Alliances françaises. On 26 October 2026 the only exam dates online were those of the Alliance française d’Antananarivo’s 2026 catalogue, for children and teenagers; it gave no exam date and no exam fee for the adult DELF and DALF.

The same catalogue lists the Alliance’s 2026 preparation courses for the DELF B1 to DALF C2 — 20 hours on four Saturdays or four weekday afternoons, from 14 February to 7 March, 16 May to 6 June, 8 August to 5 September and 24 October to 14 November — at 95,000 Ar for the B1, 99,000 Ar for the B2 and C1, and 250,000 Ar for the C2.

Where to get the dates

France Éducation international’s list of approved centres (26 October 2026) names the cultural cooperation service of the French Embassy in Antananarivo as Madagascar’s DELF-DALF body. The Alliances’ network website given in that list, alliancefr.mg, could not be reached on 26 October 2026, so the most reliable way to get the 2027 dates, the fees and the registration window is to ask the Alliance française where you want to sit the exam — in the capital, the Alliance française d’Antananarivo in Andavamamba (+261 34 80 119 95).

The 24 exam centres

France Éducation international’s list of 26 October 2026 gives 24 approved DELF-DALF centres in Madagascar, all Alliances françaises: Ambanja, Ambatondrazaka, Ambilobe, Ambositra, Antananarivo, Antsirabe, Antsiranana, Antsohihy, Fandriana, Farafangana, Fianarantsoa, Fort-Dauphin, Majunga, Manakara, Mananjary, Moramanga, Morombe, Morondava, Nosy Be, Sainte-Marie, Sambava, Tamatave, Tsiroanomandidy and Tuléar. To find the nearest one, use our test centre finder.

Adults, juniors and children

The Alliance française d’Antananarivo offers the three versions of the DELF by age: the DELF Prim from 7 to 12, the DELF junior from 13 to 17 and the DELF-DALF tout public from 17. All lead to official diplomas of the French Ministry of Education, recognised internationally and valid for life.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Madagascar as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Madagascar?

Not published online on 26 October 2026 for the adult DELF and DALF. The Alliance française d’Antananarivo announces its next DELF Prim session for April 2027; for the tout public dates, ask the Alliance française where you want to sit the exam.

When is the DELF junior in Madagascar?

In 2026, the Alliance française d’Antananarivo held it on 5–8 October, with registration from 6 July to 12 September 2026. The 2027 session had not been announced on 26 October 2026.

How much does the DELF cost in Madagascar?

The exam fees for the adult DELF and DALF were not published online on 26 October 2026. The Alliance française d’Antananarivo’s 20-hour preparation courses cost 95,000 Ar for the B1, 99,000 Ar for the B2 and C1, and 250,000 Ar for the C2.

Where can I take the DELF in Madagascar?

At one of 24 approved Alliances françaises, including Antananarivo, Antsirabe, Antsiranana, Fianarantsoa, Majunga, Tamatave, Tuléar, Nosy Be and Fort-Dauphin (France Éducation international’s list, 26 October 2026).

Can adults take the DELF in Madagascar?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public is the adult version, from 17 at the Alliance française d’Antananarivo; the DELF junior is for 13- to 17-year-olds and the DELF Prim for 7- to 12-year-olds.
