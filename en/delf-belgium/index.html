DELF exam dates 2027 in Belgium: sessions, fees and the 6 centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Belgium were not yet set on 26 October 2026: the Alliance française in Ghent announces a March session (registration from 1 December 2026 to 3 March 2027) and a June session. Before that, the DELF and DALF run from 17 to 26 November 2026 in Brussels and Ghent, with the DELF B2 on 19 November; Ghent takes registrations until 3 November, while Brussels closed on 24 October. The B2 costs €191 in Ghent; Belgium has 6 centres.

Key facts
  • 6 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Alliances françaises of Bruxelles-Europe, Antwerp and Flandre orientale (Ghent), KU Leuven Kulak in Kortrijk, the Institut supérieur des langues vivantes of the University of Liège and the European School in Mol.
  • November 2026: A1 and A2 on the 17th, B1 on the 18th, B2 on the 19th, C1 on the 24th, C2 on 26 November; speaking tests from 16 to 20 and 23 to 27 November in Brussels.
  • Registration: until 3 November 2026 in Ghent; closed on 24 October in Brussels; results at the end of December in Ghent.
  • 2027 in Ghent: March (registration from 1 December 2026 to 3 March 2027) and June (30 March to 24 May 2027); Brussels had not scheduled any 2027 session yet.
  • Fees in Ghent: A1 €117 · A2 €127 · B1 €149 · B2 €191 · C1 €238 · C2 €254; Brussels does not show its fees, 10% off for its students.
  • In 2026, Brussels held four sessions: March, June, September and November; diploma available 3 months after the exam.

Speaking tests are held in the week of the written papers: in Brussels, 16 to 20 November for A1 to B2, 23 to 27 November for the DALF. Ghent’s page shows 29 November for B2 in its calendar and the 19th in the level details; Brussels, the managing centre, gives the 19th. Antwerp, Kortrijk, Liège and Mol were not checked: their contact details are in the list.

In Ghent, you pay online when registering; no refund, level change or postponement after the deadline. The Alliance de Bruxelles-Europe did not show its fees on 26 October 2026; its students get 10% off, and the diploma is available at its reception three months after the exam.

Registration

You register online on your chosen Alliance’s website, level by level; the registration is in your name and cannot be transferred. In Ghent, test-day notices go out by email between 3 and 13 November 2026; if you have heard nothing a week before the exam, contact the centre. The diplomas are independent: you register directly for the level you want, and you need 50 points out of 100 to pass.

The 6 DELF-DALF test centres in Belgium

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Gestion Centrale DELF/DALF (Brussels) · Avenue des Arts, 46, 1000 Bruxelles · alliancefr.be. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

How to read this list. It reproduces the official list of test centres approved by France Éducation international (FEI), read on 26 October 2026: name, address, phone, generic email and website as FEI publishes them; where the listed website no longer works or has moved, we give the one we opened that day. An organisation that is not on this list is not approved.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Belgium?

They were not set on 26 October 2026. The Alliance française de Flandre orientale announces a session in March 2027 (registration from 1 December 2026 to 3 March 2027) and one in June (30 March to 24 May 2027); Bruxelles-Europe had not scheduled anything yet.

When is the next DELF exam in Belgium?

From 17 to 26 November 2026 in Brussels and Ghent: A1 and A2 on the 17th, B1 on the 18th, B2 on the 19th, DALF C1 on the 24th and C2 on 26 November. Ghent takes registrations until 3 November; in Brussels, registration closed on 24 October.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Belgium?

€191 at the Alliance française in Ghent, €172.40 for its students. The Alliance de Bruxelles-Europe did not show its fees on 26 October 2026; its students get 10% off.

Where can I take the DELF in Brussels?

At the Alliance française de Bruxelles-Europe, avenue des Arts 46, which also manages the DELF-DALF for the whole of Belgium. In 2026, it held four sessions: March, June, September and November.

When do I get my DELF diploma?

In Ghent, the November 2026 results arrive at the end of December; in Brussels, the diploma is available at reception three months after the exam, and a certificate is given when results are announced.
