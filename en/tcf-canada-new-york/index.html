TCF Canada in New York: L'Alliance New York, the fee and the 2027 dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 18 October 2026 · 5 min read

In New York, the TCF Canada is held by L'Alliance New York, the only approved TCF center in the city on France Éducation international's list — in Manhattan (22 East 60th Street) or at its Montclair campus in New Jersey. It costs $350 for members, $399 otherwise, plus a $15 administrative fee. On October 18, 2026, its booking platform showed every remaining 2026 session full — October 26, November 6, November 16 and December 14 — and six 2027 sessions open in Montclair, from January 25 to June 14.

Key facts
  • 1 approved center: L'Alliance New York, 22 East 60th Street, Manhattan; sessions also at its Montclair campus, New Jersey.
  • Fee: $350 for L'Alliance members, $399 otherwise, plus a non-refundable $15 administrative fee.
  • ⚠️ October 18, 2026: every remaining 2026 session full — Oct 26 (Montclair), Nov 6 (Manhattan), Nov 16 and Dec 14 (Montclair).
  • Open: Jan 25, Feb 22, Mar 22, Apr 19, May 17 and Jun 14, 2027, all in Montclair; no Manhattan date listed for 2027.
  • Computer-based, with a 12-minute face-to-face oral; one month between two attempts; certificate in 5 to 6 weeks.

Booking the TCF Canada in New York

L'Alliance sells the test on its booking platform, linked from its TCF Canada page on lallianceny.org — not lalliance.org, a parked domain that has nothing to do with the school. Each session has a registration deadline about two weeks before: for 2027, January 25 (by January 12), February 22 (by February 9), March 22 (by March 9), April 19 (by April 6), May 17 (by May 4) and June 14 (by June 1), all at the Montclair campus. Keep the day free — L'Alliance asks you to «be available between 9am and 6pm».

The test is computer-based for listening, reading and writing; the speaking test is «individual face-to-face … with an examiner – 3 exercises – 12 minutes». Before the deadline, a cancellation is refunded «minus an $80 cancellation fee», and a transfer costs $50. L'Alliance requires «a one-month waiting period … between two successive tests», and the certificate takes «5-6 weeks from the test date». With 2026 full, the nearest center with seats in December was Boston; other cities are on our USA page.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in New York?

At L'Alliance New York, in Manhattan (22 East 60th Street) or at its Montclair campus in New Jersey; it is the only approved TCF center in the city. In 2027, every session listed is in Montclair.

When is the next TCF Canada in New York?

On October 18, 2026, every remaining 2026 session was full (October 26, November 6 and 16, December 14). The next open dates were January 25, February 22, March 22, April 19, May 17 and June 14, 2027, in Montclair, NJ.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in New York?

$350 for L'Alliance members and $399 for non-members, plus a non-refundable $15 administrative fee (October 18, 2026).

Is lalliance.org L'Alliance New York's website?

No: lalliance.org is a parked domain for sale. L'Alliance New York's website is lallianceny.org, and its TCF Canada page links to the booking platform.

How long between two attempts in New York?

One month: «a one-month waiting period is required between two successive tests», according to L'Alliance. FEI no longer accepts re-marking requests for sessions held since September 1, 2026.
