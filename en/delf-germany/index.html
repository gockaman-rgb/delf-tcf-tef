DELF exam dates 2027 in Germany: sessions, fees and the 24 test centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

DELF exam dates 2027 in Germany: at the Instituts français, the DELF B2 written papers fall on 22 January, 12 March, 11 June, 24 September and 19 November 2027, the DALF the day after (23 September in September). Before that, the next session is on 19 and 20 November 2026 (DALF on the 21st), with registration until 8 to 13 November depending on the city. The DELF and DALF are held at 24 test centres, nine of them Instituts français sharing one online booking module; the DELF B2 costs €201, or €170.85 with the 15% discount for students of an Institut preparation course.

Key facts
  • 24 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), coordinated by the language and education cooperation office of the Institut français in Germany.
  • November 2026: A2 and B1 on the 19th, A1 and B2 on the 20th, DALF C1 and C2 on 21 November, at the Instituts français in Berlin, Bremen, Cologne, Düsseldorf, Hamburg, Leipzig, Mainz, Munich and Stuttgart.
  • 2027, DELF B2 written papers: 22 January, 12 March, 11 June, 24 September, 19 November; DALF on 23 January, 13 March, 12 June, 23 September and 20 November.
  • Instituts français fees: A1 €137 · A2 €161 · B1 €179 · B2 €201 · C1 €224 · C2 €253; 15% off after an Institut preparation course finished 4 months or less before the exam.
  • The diplomas are independent: you register directly for the level you want; you need 50 points out of 100 to pass.
  • Results within 15 working days; paper diploma about 6 months after the exam.

Speaking tests take place on dates set by each centre. The DELF junior has two sessions in the module, on 24 April and 12 June 2027; the DELF Prim, on 23 January and 5 June 2027. The other centres on the list — Aachen, Bonn, Erlangen, Frankfurt, Freiburg, Hanover, Heidelberg, Karlsruhe, Kiel, Mannheim, Rostock, Saarbrücken, Tübingen — handle their own registrations: in Mannheim, the November 2026 deadline is 6 November.

The 15% discount applies if you took an Institut français preparation course that ended four months or less before the exam. The other centres set their own fees. The DELF scolaire, at a preferential price, is organised with schools and the education ministries of the sixteen Länder.

Registration, results and diploma

At the Instituts français, you pick your session on the “Anmeldung für Sprachzertifikate” page (filter DELF or DALF, then the city), create an account and pay online; each session shows its deadline. The diplomas are independent: you can register for B2 without having taken A1, A2 or B1. Each paper is marked out of 25; you need 50 points out of 100, and at least 5 in each paper.

The centre sends the results within 15 working days of the last paper; the paper diploma arrives about 6 months after the exam, and you can ask for a certificate of achievement in the meantime.

The 24 DELF-DALF test centres, city by city

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Bureau de cooperation linguistique et éducative (Berlin) · Pariser Platz 5, 10117 Berlin · institutfrancais.de. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Germany?

At the Instituts français, the DELF B2 is on 22 January, 12 March, 11 June, 24 September and 19 November 2027, each session in four to seven cities; the DALF C1 and C2 is the day after (23 September in September).

When is the next DELF exam in Germany?

On 19 and 20 November 2026 (A2 and B1 on the 19th, A1 and B2 on the 20th) and on 21 November for the DALF, at nine Instituts français; registration until 8 to 13 November depending on the city. Then January, March, June, September and November 2027.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Germany?

€201 at the Instituts français, or €170.85 with the 15% discount given after an Institut preparation course. The DALF costs €224 (C1) and €253 (C2).

Do I need A1 before B2?

No: the DELF and DALF diplomas are independent, so you register directly for the level you want. Passing B1 does not award A1 and A2.

When do I get my DELF diploma?

Results arrive within 15 working days of the last paper; the paper diploma, about 6 months after the exam. You can ask for a certificate of achievement before that.
