TCF Canada in Germany: the test centres that offer it, fees and dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Germany, 13 centres are approved for the TCF by France Éducation international, and ten advertise the TCF Canada: the Instituts français in Berlin, Bremen, Hamburg, Mainz, Munich and Stuttgart, which sell it through a shared booking module at €295, the institute in Aachen (€295), the one in Bonn, the Franco-German Institute in Erlangen (€210) and the Institut français in Mannheim. On 26 October 2026 the module listed 23 TCF Canada sessions up to December 2027; the first with free places: Munich, on 17 November (registration until 8 November). Frankfurt, Tübingen and Dresden don’t offer it.

Key facts
  • 13 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), 10 advertising the TCF Canada: Berlin, Bremen, Hamburg, Mainz, Munich, Stuttgart, Aachen, Bonn, Erlangen and Mannheim.
  • €295 at the six Instituts français of the shared module and in Aachen; €210 in Erlangen (computer-based); Bonn and Mannheim don’t publish their fee.
  • TCF Canada sessions in 2026: Munich 17 Nov and 4 Dec, Stuttgart 27 Nov, 10 Dec and 11 Dec, Hamburg 1 Dec, Berlin 2 Dec, Bremen 7 Dec, Mainz 14 Dec; ⚠️ Berlin 4 Nov full.
  • In 2027: Berlin 20 Jan and 17 Feb; Hamburg 11 Feb, 3 Mar, 27 May, 2 Jun and 1 Jul; Mainz 18 Feb, 29 Apr, 24 Jun, 15 Jul, 21 Oct and 16 Dec.
  • No TCF Canada at Eloquia in Frankfurt (tout public, DAP, Québec, IRN), in Tübingen (no TCF on its website) or in Dresden (no session in the module).
  • Results within 15 working days; 30 days between two attempts; TCF IRN €280, TCF Québec €295.

The shared module of the Instituts français (“Anmeldung für Sprachzertifikate”) listed 112 TCF sessions on 26 October 2026, each with its own registration deadline, usually one to two weeks before the test; “Keine Plätze mehr frei” means the session is full. The TEF Canada, the other test IRCC accepts, had sessions published that day in Bonn and Erlangen on the map of Le français des affaires.

Registering for the TCF Canada in Germany

At the six Instituts français of the module, you pick a session on the “Anmeldung für Sprachzertifikate” page of the Institut français in Germany (filter TCF, then the city), create an account and pay online. In Aachen, you book online or by email, giving the version and the session; in Bonn, a registration only counts once the fee has arrived before the deadline, and it is neither refunded nor moved, except with a medical certificate; in Erlangen, you send back a PDF form.

Results arrive within 15 working days; the results certificate is digital, with a QR code and the candidate’s photo, and is valid for two years. You must wait 30 days between two attempts, whatever the version.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Germany?

At one of the ten centres that advertised it on 26 October 2026: the Instituts français in Berlin, Bremen, Hamburg, Mainz, Munich and Stuttgart (shared booking module, €295), the Institut culturel franco-allemand in Aachen (€295), the Institut français in Bonn, the Franco-German Institute in Erlangen (€210) and the Institut français in Mannheim.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Germany?

€295 at the Instituts français in Berlin, Bremen, Hamburg, Mainz, Munich and Stuttgart, as in Aachen; €210 in Erlangen. Bonn and Mannheim didn’t publish their fee on 26 October 2026.

When is the next TCF Canada in Berlin?

The 4 November 2026 session was full on 26 October; the next ones are on 2 December 2026 (registration until 24 November), then 20 January and 17 February 2027.

Can I take the TCF Canada in Frankfurt?

Not at Eloquia, Frankfurt’s only TCF centre: its website offers the TCF tout public, DAP, Québec and IRN, not the Canada version. The nearby centres that advertise it are Mainz (€295) and Mannheim.

Can I take the TCF IRN in Germany?

Yes: €280 at the Instituts français in Berlin, Hamburg and Munich, with sessions up to July 2027, and €210 in Erlangen. The results certificate is the one France Éducation international issues worldwide.
