TCF Canada in Poland: Warsaw and Kraków, sessions and registration

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Poland, the TCF is held at the Institut français de Pologne, in Warsaw and Kraków. In Warsaw, each of the 8 sessions of 2026 included the TCF Canada; the last one of the year is on 27 November 2026, with registration until 27 October, and the 2027 calendar was not yet published on 26 October 2026. Kraków also presents the TCF Canada. Fees are not online; you register with a downloadable form.

Key facts
  • 2 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), the two branches of the Institut français de Pologne.
  • Warsaw: 8 sessions in 2026, all with the TCF Canada, alternating with the TCF Québec or the TCF IRN; registration closes one month before.
  • Last 2026 session: 27 Nov (tout public, DAP, IRN, Canada), registration until 27 Oct.
  • Registration: form to download from the Institut’s TCF Canada page; fee not published.
  • Kraków: TCF Canada, Québec, IRN, tout public and DAP pages; dates not published.
  • TEF Canada, the other test IRCC accepts: sessions published by the Alliance française in Łódź on 26 October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Poland?

At the Institut français de Pologne, in Warsaw (Plac Europejski 3) or Kraków: they are the two approved TCF centres, and both present the Canada version.

When is the next TCF Canada in Warsaw?

On 27 November 2026, with registration until 27 October 2026. The 2027 calendar was not published on 26 October 2026; in 2026, the Institut held 8 sessions, all with the TCF Canada.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Poland?

The Institut français did not publish its fee on 26 October 2026: it appears on the registration form or can be obtained from the exams office.

Where can I take the TEF Canada in Poland?

The map of Le français des affaires showed TEF Canada sessions at the Alliance française in Łódź on 26 October 2026.
