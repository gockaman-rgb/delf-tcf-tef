TCF Canada in Greece: Athens and Thessaloniki, dates and registration

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Greece, the TCF Canada is held at the Institut français de Grèce in Athens: its 2026-2027 calendar brings together the TCF tout public, TCF Québec, TCF Canada and TCF IRN on 19 November 2026 — registration from 2 to 5 November only —, then on 21 January, 25 February, 24 March, 22 April and 17 June 2027. The Institut français in Thessaloniki presents the TCF at €100, without mentioning the Canada version. In Athens, each registration window lasts only four days.

Key facts
  • 2 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Instituts français in Athens and Thessaloniki, on computer.
  • Athens: 6 TCF sessions, TCF Canada included, from November 2026 to June 2027; a 4-day registration window, set for each session.
  • Next: 19 Nov 2026, registration 2 to 5 Nov; then 21 Jan 2027, registration 30 Nov to 3 Dec 2026.
  • 2027: 25 Feb (registration 1 to 4 Feb), 24 Mar (1 to 4 Mar), 22 Apr (5 to 8 Apr), 17 Jun (31 May to 3 Jun).
  • TCF Canada fee not published in Athens; Thessaloniki shows €100 for the TCF, without specifying the version.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Greece?

At the Institut français de Grèce, 31 Sina Street in Athens: its 2026-2027 calendar includes the TCF Canada at every session. The Institut français in Thessaloniki presents the TCF without specifying the Canada version.

When is the next TCF Canada in Athens?

On 19 November 2026, with registration from 2 to 5 November only; then on 21 January 2027 (registration from 30 November to 3 December 2026).

What are the 2027 TCF dates in Greece?

In Athens: 21 January, 25 February, 24 March, 22 April and 17 June 2027, each with a four-day registration window.

How much does the TCF cost in Greece?

Thessaloniki shows €100 for the TCF; the Institut français in Athens does not publish its fee in its calendar.
