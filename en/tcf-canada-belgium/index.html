TCF Canada in Belgium: the two test centres, fees and dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Belgium, two centres are approved for the TCF, and both run the TCF Canada, on computer: the Alliance française de Bruxelles-Europe (€268, next session on Wednesday 9 December 2026) and the Alliance française de Flandre orientale in Ghent (€260, three sessions a year, with no 2027 calendar published on 26 October 2026). You book and pay online, on each Alliance’s website; the speaking test takes place on the same day as the written tests.

Key facts
  • 2 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), both offering the TCF Canada, on computer.
  • Brussels: TCF Canada €268 on 9 December 2026; TCF tout public €258 on 10 December, TCF IRN €220 on 14 December.
  • Ghent: TCF Canada €260, tout public €250; three sessions a year (February, May, August-September); 2027 not published.
  • Brussels hands out a provisional certificate on the day; Ghent shows the multiple-choice results at the end of the test, the full results within 15 working days.
  • 30 days between two attempts; every registration is in the candidate’s name.
  • DELF-DALF: 6 centres in Belgium; the November 2026 session is closed, and 2027 sessions are not yet scheduled.

In Brussels, a request for a particular speaking-test time goes to the exams office before registration closes; candidates are photographed on the test day. In Ghent, a cancellation after the deadline is only refunded with proof (a medical certificate, for example), minus an administration fee of about €2.

The DELF and DALF in Belgium

The DELF and DALF are held at 6 centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026) — the Alliances françaises of Bruxelles-Europe, Antwerp and Flandre orientale, KU Leuven Kulak in Kortrijk, the Institut supérieur des langues vivantes of the University of Liège and the European School in Mol —, under the central management of the Alliance de Bruxelles-Europe. It holds four tout public sessions a year: in 2026, March, June, September and November, with written papers from 17 to 26 November and registration closed on 24 October. On 26 October 2026, no 2027 session was on sale yet and fees were not shown; the Alliance’s students get 10% off.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Belgium?

At the Alliance française de Bruxelles-Europe, avenue des Arts 46 (€268), or at the Alliance française de Flandre orientale, Henleykaai 86 in Ghent (€260): they are the country’s two approved TCF centres, and both offer the Canada version, on computer.

When is the next TCF Canada in Brussels?

On Wednesday 9 December 2026, from 10:00 to 12:40 for the written tests, with the speaking test in the afternoon. Ghent had not published its 2027 sessions on 26 October 2026.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Belgium?

€268 in Brussels and €260 in Ghent. The TCF tout public costs €258 in Brussels and €250 in Ghent, the TCF IRN €220 in Brussels.

When will I get my results?

In Brussels, a provisional certificate is handed out on the test day and the France Éducation international certificate arrives within two weeks; in Ghent, the full results arrive within 15 working days.

Can I take the DELF in Brussels in 2027?

Yes, but the 2027 sessions were not yet scheduled on 26 October 2026. In 2026, the Alliance de Bruxelles-Europe held four: March, June, September and November.
