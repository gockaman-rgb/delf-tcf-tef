TCF Canada in Los Angeles: the Alliance Française, Pasadena, the fees and the dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 18 October 2026 · 5 min read

In Los Angeles, the TCF Canada is held by the Alliance Française de Los Angeles (10390 Santa Monica Blvd, Suite 300) for $505 — and, nearby, by the Alliance Française de Pasadena for $460. On October 18, 2026, Los Angeles listed November 20 and December 18, then one session a month from January 22 to June 11, 2027, none marked full; you apply by email at least 15 days before the test. Pasadena had a single date, November 12 (deadline October 20).

Key facts
  • Alliance Française de Los Angeles: TCF Canada $505 (the same fee for the TCF tout public, IRN, Québec and DAP); no member rate shown.
  • Los Angeles: Nov 20 (apply by Nov 6) and Dec 18 (by Dec 4); 2027: Jan 22, Feb 26, Mar 19, Apr 23, May 21, Jun 11. Oct 23 closed on Oct 9.
  • Apply by email to tcf@afdela.org «no later than 15 days before the exam date»; pay on receipt of your written convocation.
  • Pasadena: Nov 12, 2026 (deadline Oct 20), $460 «Registration fees included», plus 3.5% for card payments; not refundable once registered.
  • ⚠️ The official sites are afdela.org and afdepasadena.org — afla.org and afpasadena.org belong to other organizations.

Applying in Los Angeles or Pasadena

The Alliance Française de Los Angeles doesn't sell the test online: you «email your application to our TCF administrator at tcf@afdela.org — no later than 15 days before the exam date», and «payment must be made upon receipt of your written convocation». The test takes place at the Alliance, 10390 Santa Monica Blvd, Suite 300; reading and listening results are given «as soon as you finish the test». Its page doesn't say how long you must wait between two attempts.

In Pasadena (232 N. Lake Avenue), the Alliance Française listed one TCF Canada session on October 18, 2026: November 12, with registration until October 20, for $460 including registration fees, plus a non-refundable 3.5% for card payments. «The exam fee is not refundable once registered», and results arrive «within four to six weeks of the test date», by email. No 2027 session was listed. Further south, San Diego was sold out; other states are on our USA page.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Los Angeles?

At the Alliance Française de Los Angeles (10390 Santa Monica Blvd, Suite 300), or nearby at the Alliance Française de Pasadena (232 N. Lake Avenue); both are approved by France Éducation international.

When is the next TCF Canada in Los Angeles?

On October 18, 2026: November 20 (apply by November 6) and December 18 (by December 4), then January 22, February 26, March 19, April 23, May 21 and June 11, 2027. Pasadena: November 12, deadline October 20.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Los Angeles?

$505 at the Alliance Française de Los Angeles and $460 at the Alliance Française de Pasadena, plus 3.5% for card payments there (October 18, 2026).

How do I register in Los Angeles?

By email to tcf@afdela.org, at least 15 days before the exam date; you pay once you receive your written convocation.
