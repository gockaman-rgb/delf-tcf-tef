DELF exam dates 2027 in Sweden: sessions, fees and the 26 centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Sweden were not yet published on 26 October 2026. The Institut français de Suède runs the DELF and DALF tout public three times a year in Stockholm, Uppsala, Borås and Växjö (Gothenburg in spring): in 2026, 11–13 March, 6–8 May and 3–5 November, with registration closed on 9 October. The DELF B2 costs SEK 1,450. Sweden has no TCF centre: for Canada, the Institut runs the TEF Canada (SEK 2,900).

Key facts
  • 26 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), mostly schools; for the tout public, the Institut français de Suède lists Stockholm, Uppsala, Borås, Växjö and Gothenburg.
  • 2026: 11–13 March (registration 30 January to 13 February), 6–8 May (27 March – 10 April), 3–5 November (25 September – 9 October).
  • Fees since 1 January 2026: A1 SEK 800 · A2 SEK 900 · B1 SEK 950 · B2 SEK 1,450 · C1 SEK 2,500 · C2 SEK 2,800.
  • Stockholm (Institut français) and Uppsala (university): A1 to C2; Borås and Växjö: A1 to B2; Gothenburg (Hvitfeldtska): B1, B2 and C1 in spring.
  • No TCF centre in Sweden: TEF Canada at the Institut français de Suède, SEK 2,900, next session on 25 November 2026 (registration 28 October to 11 November).
  • Nearest TCF Canada: Fransk kulturhus in Oslo (NOK 3,150); Institut français du Danemark in Copenhagen.

The DELF-DALF tout public is for those aged 18 and over; younger candidates take the DELF Prim or DELF junior, presented on a separate page of the Institut.

No TCF in Sweden: the TEF Canada and nearby centres

No TCF centre is approved in Sweden (FEI list of 26 October 2026). For a Canadian application, the Institut français de Suède runs the other test IRCC accepts, the TEF Canada: SEK 2,900, plus SEK 100 registration; next session on 25 November 2026, registration from 28 October to 11 November 2026; five sessions in 2026 (January, April, June, September, November). The TEF for French citizenship costs SEK 2,000 there.

The nearest TCF Canada is in Oslo, at Fransk kulturhus (NOK 3,150, several sessions a year, register at least 5 weeks ahead), or at the Institut français du Danemark in Copenhagen, whose 30 October 2026 TCF Canada session closed registration on 18 September; the TCF tout public and TCF IRN are held there on 15 and 16 March 2027.

The 26 DELF-DALF test centres, city by city

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Gestion Centrale DELF/DALF (Stockholm) · Kommendörsgatan 13 BOX 5296, 10246 Stockholm · ifsuede.com. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Sweden?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026, the Institut français de Suède held three sessions — 11–13 March, 6–8 May and 3–5 November —, each with a two-week registration window closing a little less than a month before.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Sweden?

SEK 1,450 at the Institut français de Suède (fees in force since 1 January 2026); SEK 950 for B1, SEK 2,500 for the DALF C1.

Can I take the TCF Canada in Sweden?

No: no TCF centre is approved in Sweden. The Institut français de Suède runs the TEF Canada (SEK 2,900, session on 25 November 2026); the nearest TCF Canada is in Oslo (Fransk kulturhus, NOK 3,150) or Copenhagen.

Where can I take the DELF outside Stockholm?

At Uppsala University (A1 to C2), in Borås (Bodaskolan) and Växjö (Linnaeus University), A1 to B2, and in Gothenburg (Hvitfeldtska gymnasiet) for B1, B2 and C1 in spring.
