TCF Canada in Boston: the International School of Boston, the fee and the dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 18 October 2026 · 5 min read

Near Boston, the TCF Canada is held by the International School of Boston — formerly the Lycée International de Boston —, 45 Matignon Road in Cambridge, the only approved TCF center in Massachusetts. It costs $350, on Saturdays: on October 18, 2026, the next sessions were November 21 (deadline November 17) and December 12 (deadline December 8), and a January Saturday was listed. A weekday date is possible for $60 more.

Key facts
  • 1 approved center: the International School of Boston (ISB Extension, open to adults), 45 Matignon Road, Cambridge, MA.
  • TCF Canada $350; a weekday date costs $60 more (deadline a week before the test).
  • Saturdays: November 21 (deadline November 17) and December 12 (deadline December 8); October 10 was full, with a waitlist.
  • Also listed: «Saturday, January 9th, 2026 (Deadline January 5th)» — January 9 falls on a Saturday in 2027: confirm the year with the school.
  • Registration and payment by card on the school's online form; results certificate valid for two years.

Booking the TCF Canada in Boston

The school registers candidates through a TCF Test Registration form on its Extension pages (isbos.org): you choose the session and the test — «TCF Canada ($350)» — and pay by card. Besides the Saturday dates, the form offers a weekday date for $60 more (state your preferred date in the comments, at least a week ahead) and a private Saturday session for $100 more; a $100 retake fee applies only to excused candidates, and returning ISB candidates may get $50 off, subject to eligibility. The form doesn't say whether the test is on paper or on computer, nor how long you must wait between two attempts: ask the school, at the general Extension address adulted@isbos.org. Other centers and dates: our USA page and New York.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Boston?

At the International School of Boston (formerly the Lycée International de Boston), 45 Matignon Road in Cambridge, the only approved TCF center in Massachusetts.

When is the next TCF Canada in Boston?

On October 18, 2026: Saturday, November 21 (deadline November 17) and Saturday, December 12 (deadline December 8). A January 9 session was also listed, dated 2026 on the form — January 9 is a Saturday in 2027.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Boston?

$350 for a Saturday session; a weekday date costs $60 more, and a private Saturday session $100 more (October 18, 2026).

Can I take the TCF Canada in Boston on a weekday?

Yes, for $60 more: indicate your preferred date in the comments of the registration form, at least a week before the test.
