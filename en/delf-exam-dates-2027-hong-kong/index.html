DELF exam dates 2027 in Hong Kong: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 4 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Hong Kong were not yet published on 26 October 2026. The Alliance Française de Hong Kong — the only accredited DELF-DALF centre in Hong Kong — held adult sessions in March, April and November 2026; the next exam is the DELF Junior on 1–8 December 2026, with registration open until 30 October. You book online on afhongkong.org or at the Jordan, Shatin or Wanchai reception.

Key facts
  • 2027: adult dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026).
  • 2026 adult sessions: March (A1 to B1), April (B2 to C2), November (all levels, 3–18 November).
  • DELF Junior: 1–8 December 2026, registration 30 September – 30 October.
  • Adult fees: A1 HK$1,500 · A2 1,700 · B1 2,100 · B2 2,300 · C1 3,100 · C2 3,300.
  • Results by e-mail about 10 weeks after the exam; the diploma about four months later.
  • HKDSE: French as a Category C subject needs a DELF A2 or above taken in the two years before.

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

The adult sessions are held over several days, each level with its own written-paper day; A1, A2 and B1 may be held in a single day, depending on numbers. The 2027 dates had not been published on 26 October 2026; in 2026 the pattern was a spring split into two sessions and an autumn session for every level.

DELF Junior and the HKDSE

Secondary-school students (12 to 18) take the DELF Junior. It is the official language examination for French as a Category C subject of the HKDSE: you need a DELF A2 or above taken within the two years before your HKDSE — for 2027 candidates, in 2025 or 2026. The December 2026 session is the last junior session of 2026.

How to register

The adult DELF-DALF is designed for adults, with no age limit for the general public. Register online with the “Register” button of your level while the session is open, or at the reception of any of the three centres — Jordan, Shatin or Wanchai. Your registration is valid only once you have the Alliance’s confirmation e-mail; an exam notice follows about ten days before the exam. Fees are neither refundable nor transferable, so check that you are free on every day of your level. Macao has its own approved centre, the Alliance française de Macao (France Éducation international’s list, 26 October 2026).

Results and diplomas

Every paper is double-marked by two independent examiners and checked by the DELF-DALF jury of Hong Kong and Macau. Results are sent by e-mail about ten weeks after the exam. The diploma, issued by the French Ministry of Education, takes about four more months; you collect it at the Jordan centre, or have it sent by courier at your own cost.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Hong Kong as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Hong Kong?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026, the Alliance Française de Hong Kong held adult sessions in March (A1 to B1), April (B2 to C2) and November (all levels); watch afhongkong.org for the 2027 calendar.

Is there still a DELF session in Hong Kong in 2026?

Yes, for secondary-school students: the DELF Junior on 1–8 December 2026, with registration until 30 October 2026. The November 2026 adult session closed its registration on 3 October.

How much is the DELF B2 in Hong Kong?

HK$2,300 for adults, HK$1,800 for the DELF Junior B2 (afhongkong.org, 26 October 2026).

Can adults take the DELF in Hong Kong?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public is designed for adults, with no age limit for the general public. Secondary-school students take the DELF Junior, primary pupils the DELF Prim.

Which DELF level do I need for the HKDSE?

DELF A2 or above, taken within the two years before your HKDSE: for 2027 candidates, in 2025 or 2026. The DELF Junior is the version for secondary-school students.
