TCF Canada in Bhopal: the Alliance Française, the fee and the dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 17 October 2026 · 5 min read

Booking in Bhopal

You book and pay in the Alliance’s online shop, with a scanned photo ID (passport or Aadhaar), and registration closes one month before each date. On test day, arriving late or without your original photo ID means no refund. Leave 30 days between two attempts; results take four to eight weeks. The Alliance also offers the TEF Canada.
