TCF Canada in Abu Dhabi: the Alliance Française, the fee and the dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 18 October 2026 · 5 min read

In Abu Dhabi, the TCF Canada is held by the Alliance Française Abu Dhabi — listed by FEI as the «Institut français – Antenne AFAD» — at 99 Al Qouʻ Street in Al Bateen, on computer, for 1,800 AED. On 18 October 2026, two sessions were open: Friday 20 November (registration until 9 November) and Wednesday 16 December (until 6 December). Its Al Ain branch held no TCF Canada session.

Key facts
  • 1 approved centre in Abu Dhabi: the Alliance Française Abu Dhabi, 99 Al Qouʻ St, Al Bateen; Al Ain is on FEI's list too, but showed no TCF Canada session.
  • TCF Canada 1,800 AED, «Exam done at our center on computer»; TCF IRN 1,380 AED.
  • Open on 18 October 2026: Friday 20 November (deadline 9 November) and Wednesday 16 December (deadline 6 December).
  • Register online or at the Alliance's offices, during the registration period only; a passport copy is required.
  • ⚠️ Fees «will NOT be refunded or transferred under ANY circumstances» once registration is done.

Booking the TCF Canada in Abu Dhabi

The Alliance sells its sessions on its online booking platform, linked from its TCF Canada page (afabudhabi.org), and at its offices: «Candidates can register only during the registration period online or by visiting our offices», and «Seats are limited … first-come first-serve basis». Bring a copy of your passport. Once registered, the fee is neither refunded nor transferred. All its TCF sessions are held at the Al Bateen centre — the TCF tout public on 18 November, the TCF IRN on 19 November, the TCF Canada on 20 November — and none at Al Ain. In Dubai, the Alliance Française also holds the TCF Canada at 1,800 AED: TCF Canada in Dubai and the UAE.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Abu Dhabi?

At the Alliance Française Abu Dhabi (FEI lists it as «Institut français – Antenne AFAD»), 99 Al Qouʻ Street, Al Bateen, on computer.

When is the next TCF Canada in Abu Dhabi?

On 18 October 2026: Friday 20 November (registration until 9 November) and Wednesday 16 December (until 6 December).

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Abu Dhabi?

1,800 AED, the same as in Dubai; the TCF IRN costs 1,380 AED (18 October 2026).

Can I take the TCF Canada in Al Ain?

Not on 18 October 2026: the Al Ain branch of the Alliance Française Abu Dhabi listed no TCF Canada session; all TCF sessions were at the Al Bateen centre.
