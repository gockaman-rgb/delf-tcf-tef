TCF Canada in Romania: the three Instituts français, sessions and registration

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Romania, the three centres approved for the TCF are branches of the Institut français — Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca and Timișoara —, and all three present the TCF Canada, on computer. Timișoara publishes a calendar: 4 November (registration until 27 October), 18 November, 9 and 16 December 2026. In Cluj-Napoca, you set your date with the office at least 10 working days ahead; Bucharest refers candidates to its registration office. None of the three published its fee on 26 October 2026.

Key facts
  • 3 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), all Instituts français, all computer-based, all with the TCF Canada on offer.
  • Timișoara: sessions on 4 Nov (deadline 27 Oct), 18 Nov (10 Nov), 9 Dec (1 Dec) and 16 Dec 2026 (8 Dec).
  • Cluj-Napoca: date by appointment, register at least 10 working days ahead; test-day notice by email two days before.
  • Fees not published: ask the Institut.
  • The four versions are presented everywhere: tout public, Canada, Québec, IRN.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Romania?

At the Institut français, in one of its three approved branches: Bucharest, Cluj-Napoca or Timișoara. All three present the TCF Canada, on computer.

When is the next TCF in Timișoara?

On 4 November 2026 (registration until 27 October), then 18 November, 9 and 16 December 2026.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Romania?

The Instituts français did not publish their fee on 26 October 2026: ask the registration office.

How do I register in Cluj-Napoca?

Agree on a date with the Institut’s office, at least 10 working days ahead; the test-day notice arrives by email two days before the exam.
