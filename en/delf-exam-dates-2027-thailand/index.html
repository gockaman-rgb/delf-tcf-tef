DELF exam dates 2027 in Thailand: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Thailand were not yet published on 26 October 2026. The Alliance Française de Bangkok, the only approved centre, held three sessions in 2026: adults on Sunday 8 March, Sunday 7 June and Sunday 1 November, juniors and children the day before. The November session closed its registration on 27 September. Calendar, fees and results: afthailande.org.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026).
  • Next adult exam: Sunday 1 November 2026; registration (24 August – 27 September) is closed.
  • 2026 registration windows: 5 January – 8 February, 23 March – 6 May, 24 August – 27 September.
  • Fees: A1 2,600 THB · A2 2,800 · B1 3,400 · B2 3,600 · C1 4,900 · C2 5,200; 20% off for AF Bangkok students.
  • DELF junior and Prim on the Saturday before; a DELF scolaire session in January for Thai schools.
  • One centre: Alliance Française de Bangkok, 179 Witthayu Road, Lumphini.

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

The Alliance Française de Bangkok holds the DELF-DALF on weekends: the adult (tout public) exams on a Sunday, the DELF junior and DELF Prim on the Saturday before. The March and June sessions offer every version and level; the November session has no DELF Prim. The 2027 calendar had not been published on 26 October 2026.

How to register

Register with the Alliance Française de Bangkok during the published window; its exam page gives the windows, the fees and an exam-office address for questions (examen.bangkok@afthailande.org). For the January DELF scolaire, schools register their pupils: the registration is made by the teacher. The results of each session are posted on the same page — those of March and June 2026 are online as PDFs: afthailande.org.

Adults, juniors and schools

Adults sit the DELF-DALF tout public, A1 to C2. The DELF junior is for secondary-school teenagers and the DELF Prim for primary-school children aged 8 to 12; the DELF scolaire, in January, is reserved for Thai schools. All lead to the same diplomas, valid for life.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Thailand as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Thailand?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026 the Alliance Française de Bangkok held adult exams on Sunday 8 March, Sunday 7 June and Sunday 1 November, with juniors and children on the Saturday before; the 2027 calendar will appear on afthailande.org.

When is the next DELF exam in Thailand?

Sunday 1 November 2026 for adults, Saturday 31 October 2026 for the DELF junior, at the Alliance Française de Bangkok. Registration closed on 27 September 2026.

How much is the DELF B2 in Thailand?

3,600 THB at the Alliance Française de Bangkok, with 20% off for its students. A1 costs 2,600 THB, A2 2,800, B1 3,400, DALF C1 4,900 and C2 5,200 THB.

Can adults take the DELF in Thailand?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public, from A1 to C2, is for adults. Teenagers can take the DELF junior and children the DELF Prim; the DELF scolaire is for Thai schools only.

How do I book the DELF in Thailand?

With the Alliance Française de Bangkok, the only approved centre, during the registration window — in 2026, five to six weeks long and closing four to five weeks before the exam.
