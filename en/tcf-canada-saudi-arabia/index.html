TCF Canada in Saudi Arabia: the centres, the fee and the next sessions

By Augusto Grone · Updated 13 October 2026 · 5 min read

اقرأ بالعربية

Yes, you can take the TCF Canada in Saudi Arabia. On 13 October 2026, France Éducation international listed 3 approved TCF centres in the country, all run by the Alliance française d’Arabie Saoudite (AFAS): Al Khobar, Jeddah and Riyadh. Its booking platform showed a TCF Canada in Jeddah on Tuesday 20 October, on computer, and an e-TCF Canada in Riyadh on Thursday 12 November, already marked “Full” — both at 1,950 SAR; the Jeddah listing states “No postponement, no refund, no change of date allowed.”

Key facts
  • 3 approved TCF centres (FEI list of 13 October 2026), all Alliance française d’Arabie Saoudite: Al Khobar, Jeddah, Riyadh; computer sessions in Jeddah and Riyadh.
  • Fee: 1,950 SAR for the TCF Canada, the e-TCF Canada, the TCF tout public and the e-TEF Canada.
  • Jeddah: TCF Canada on Tuesday 20 October 2026, 10:00 to 15:00, computer-based.
  • Riyadh: e-TCF Canada on Thursday 12 November 2026 at the Ar-Rabih venue — full on 13 October; registration closes 7 working days before the test.
  • Jeddah TCF Canada: “No postponement, no refund, no change of date allowed.” We did not find the results delay on the AFAS site or platform.

You register online, on the AFAS platform linked from the exams page of af-ksa.org. Read the conditions before paying: the Jeddah TCF Canada listing states “No postponement, no refund, no change of date allowed”, and for the e-TCF Canada, registration closes 7 working days before the test date — earlier if all places are taken. One detail to know: the Ar-Rabih venue where AFAS schedules its e-TCF and e-TEF Canada sessions in Riyadh is not on France Éducation international’s list, which gives the AFAS Riyadh centre at Al Kindi Plaza (Gate 14, As-Safarat); ask AFAS if in doubt. No TCF session was listed for Al Khobar on 13 October.

Elsewhere in the Gulf and the Levant

If the Saudi sessions are full, the nearest approved TCF centres on France Éducation international’s list of 13 October 2026 are in Bahrain (Alliance française, Manama), Qatar (Institut français, Doha), Kuwait (Institut français) and Jordan (Institut français, Amman) — one each; we did not check whether they offer the TCF Canada itself, so check their websites. Oman has no TCF centre on the list. The United Arab Emirates have four centres, two of them in Dubai; see also Egypt and Lebanon.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take the TCF Canada in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. The Alliance française d’Arabie Saoudite runs it: on 13 October 2026 its platform listed a TCF Canada in Jeddah on Tuesday 20 October (computer-based) and an e-TCF Canada in Riyadh on Thursday 12 November, which was already full.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Saudi Arabia?

1,950 SAR, as published on the AFAS booking platform on 13 October 2026 — the same fee as the TCF tout public and the e-TEF Canada.

Can I get a refund or change my date?

Not for the Jeddah TCF Canada: its listing states “No postponement, no refund, no change of date allowed.” For the e-TCF Canada, registration closes 7 working days before the test.

Is the TEF Canada available in Saudi Arabia?

AFAS listed an e-TEF Canada in Riyadh on Thursday 19 November 2026, at 1,950 SAR; it was full on 13 October.

Is a TCF Canada taken in Saudi Arabia accepted by IRCC?

Yes: the results certificate is issued by France Éducation international whichever approved centre you test at, and it is valid for two years.
