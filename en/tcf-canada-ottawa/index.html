Taking the TCF Canada in Ottawa: the two approved centres, the next dates and booking

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Ottawa, you can take the TCF Canada at 2 centres approved by France Éducation international, both computer-based: the Alliance française Ottawa (352 MacLaren Street), which also offers it on paper, and FrancoLangues in Kanata. On 26 October 2026, the Alliance charged $400 and had seats on 10 November 2026 and on 6 and 8 January 2027 (computer), and on 14 January 2027 (paper). It is strict on test day — a valid passport and the printed test-day notice, with no exceptions — and sends results by email within two to three weeks. Below: the centres, their contacts, and how to book.

Key facts
  • 2 approved centres: Alliance française Ottawa and FrancoLangues (Kanata).
  • Alliance française Ottawa: $400; open on 26 October 2026: 10 November 2026, 6 and 8 January 2027 (computer), 14 January 2027 (paper); further January dates on sale from 1 November.
  • Alliance française Ottawa: TCF Canada computer-based or paper-based; a valid passport and the printed test-day notice are mandatory, with no exceptions.
  • FrancoLangues (105 Schneider Road, Kanata): booking through its online appointment page; no refunds or date changes; 30 days between two tests.
  • Results by email within 2 to 3 weeks; no duplicate of the results certificate.
  • Accommodations: request them before you register, with medical documentation.
  • FEI has dropped re-marking for sessions held since 1 September 2026.

Booking the next sessions in Ottawa

The Alliance française Ottawa lists its TCF Canada sessions on its TCF page, each with its price and either an «Add to cart» button or the date its sales open; «No dates will be communicated by email or phone», so the page is the only place to watch. On 26 October 2026, the computer-based sessions of 10 November 2026 and 6 and 8 January 2027 were open, the paper-based session of 14 January 2027 too, and later January dates were to go on sale on 1 November 2026 — all at $400.

FrancoLangues, at 105 Schneider Road in Kanata, takes TCF bookings through its online appointment page and gives its fee when you book. Its rules are strict: «No refunds or test date changes will be accepted under any circumstances», 30 days between two tests, and the certificate arrives four to eight weeks after the test. Every centre in the province: TCF Canada in Ontario.

How to register in Ottawa

In Canada, each centre handles its own registrations online, on its own website, with the passport you’ll use for your IRCC application. Registration is final, and cancelling costs money: the Alliance Française de Montréal keeps $75 if you cancel more than 15 days ahead, and refunds nothing after that. In the big cities, sessions fill up within minutes: create your account in advance and log in the moment registration opens. Allow at least 20 days between two attempts, whichever centre you test at. Results arrive by email within 2 to 4 weeks and are valid for two years. On test day, bring a valid passport and your printed test-day notice (admission letter).

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Ottawa?

At the Alliance française Ottawa (352 MacLaren Street), computer-based or on paper, or at FrancoLangues in Kanata; both are approved.

What should I bring on test day in Ottawa?

A valid passport and your printed test-day notice: the Alliance française refuses entry without both, with no exceptions, and checks identity throughout the test.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Ottawa?

$400 at the Alliance française Ottawa, for the computer-based or the paper-based test (26 October 2026); on 17 September its TCF page showed no fee. FrancoLangues gives its fee when you book. For reference: $390 in Vancouver, $400 in Edmonton, $440 at UQTR.

When is the next TCF Canada in Ottawa?

On 26 October 2026, the Alliance française Ottawa had seats on 10 November 2026 and on 6 and 8 January 2027 (computer), and on 14 January 2027 (paper); further January sessions were to go on sale on 1 November.

How do I book the TCF at FrancoLangues in Kanata?

Through FrancoLangues’ online appointment page, linked from its TCF page; the test takes place at 105 Schneider Road, Kanata. No refunds or date changes are accepted, and you must wait 30 days between two tests.
