DELF exam dates 2027 in Egypt: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Egypt (مواعيد امتحان DELF 2027) were not yet published on 26 October 2026. The autumn 2026 session of the Institut français d’Égypte starts on 7 November: DALF C1 on 7 November, C2 on the 12th, A2 on the 14th, A1 on the 19th, B2 on the 21st and B1 on the 28th (registration closed on 1 October). In 2026 the IFE held two adult sessions, June–July and November; you register with its online form: ifegypte.com.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026).
  • November 2026: C1 Sat 7 · C2 Thu 12 · A2 Sat 14 · A1 Thu 19 · B2 Sat 21 · B1 Sat 28 November; registration 20 September – 1 October (closed).
  • 2 adult sessions in 2026: 20 June – 11 July (registration 3–14 May) and 7–28 November.
  • Fees 2026: A1 2,405 EGP · A2 3,055 · B1 3,900 · B2 5,265 · C1 7,280 · C2 7,605; reduced rate 1,690 to 5,330 EGP.
  • DELF Junior: March–April and August 2026, same fees as the adult A1 to B2.
  • 3 centres: Institut français d’Égypte in Cairo (Mounira), Heliopolis and Alexandria.

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

The Institut français d’Égypte (IFE), Egypt’s national DELF-DALF coordinator, gives each level its own exam day, on a Thursday or a Saturday, spread over three weeks. In 2026 it held two sessions of the DELF tout public and DALF, each with a registration window of about two weeks. The 2027 dates had not been published on 26 October 2026.

How to register

When a session is open, the IFE’s “Prochaines sessions” page links to an online registration form for the DELF tout public and DALF, with the brochure of dates and fees. You need a photocopy of your identity card or, preferably, your passport. According to the IFE, the DELF B2 is required to apply through the Études en France platform, and the DALF C1 opens French-speaking university programmes.

DELF Junior and DELF Prim

The IFE runs separate sessions for school pupils. In 2026 the DELF Junior was held on 7 March (A1), 14 March (A2), 28 March (B1) and 18 April (B2), with registration in December 2025 and January 2026, then on 15 August (A1), 20 August (A2), 22 August (B2) and 29 August (B1), with registration from 6 to 16 July. Schools register with a copy of each pupil’s birth certificate; individual candidates add a recent photo and a school certificate. The IFE’s page announced a DELF Prim session in September 2026.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Egypt as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Egypt (مواعيد امتحان DELF 2027)?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026 the Institut français d’Égypte held two adult sessions: 20 June to 11 July (registration 3–14 May) and 7 to 28 November (registration 20 September – 1 October).

When is the next DELF exam in Egypt?

The autumn 2026 session: DALF C1 on 7 November, C2 on 12 November, DELF A2 on 14 November, A1 on 19 November, B2 on 21 November and B1 on 28 November 2026. Registration closed on 1 October 2026.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Egypt?

5,265 EGP at the full rate and 3,705 EGP at the reduced rate (2026). A1 costs 2,405 EGP, A2 3,055, B1 3,900, DALF C1 7,280 and C2 7,605 EGP.

Can adults take the DELF in Egypt?

Yes: the DELF tout public and the DALF are for adults and students; pupils have their own DELF Junior and DELF Prim sessions.

How do I book the DELF in Egypt?

With the Institut français d’Égypte’s online form, linked from its “Prochaines sessions” page while a session is open, with a photocopy of your ID card or passport. The exams are held in Cairo (Mounira, Heliopolis) and Alexandria.
