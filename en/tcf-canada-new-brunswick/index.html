TCF Canada in New Brunswick: Moncton, Edmundston and Bathurst — dates, fees, booking

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

New Brunswick has three approved TCF centres — the Alliance française de Moncton and the CCNB (Collège communautaire du Nouveau-Brunswick) campuses in Edmundston and Bathurst — and, unlike Vancouver, Calgary or Halifax, they had seats on 26 October 2026. The CCNB was selling TCF Canada sessions on weekends until 20 December, for $390; the Alliance in Moncton, ten dates from 1 December 2026 to 9 January 2027, also at $390. All on computer, with the speaking test in front of an examiner.

Key facts
  • 3 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Alliance française de Moncton and the CCNB campuses in Edmundston and Bathurst.
  • $390 at all three; on computer, speaking test with a jury.
  • CCNB: sessions every weekend from 31 October to 20 December 2026 in Edmundston (Saturday and Sunday) and Bathurst; on 26 October, all but two had seats left — Bathurst on 31 October and Edmundston on 1 November were full.
  • Moncton: 1, 4, 8, 9, 11, 15 and 18 December 2026, 6, 8 and 9 January 2027, all open for booking on 26 October.
  • 20 days between two attempts in Moncton, 30 days at the CCNB; certificates in about 15 working days.
  • No refunds; Moncton accepts one free date change up to 7 days before, requested by email.

How to book in New Brunswick

The CCNB sells its TCF sessions on an online booking page (Bookeo), linked from its TCF page by «Inscrivez-vous ici!»: each date shows the campus, the number of seats left and the price, $390. The reading, listening and writing tests are on computer, the speaking test «en présence d'un jury»; allow 30 days between two sessions, and about 15 days for the results certificate. The CCNB's pages are in French; the toll-free number is 1-800-376-5353.

In Moncton, the Alliance française sells its TCF Canada dates in its online store: «aucun remboursement ne sera accordé et l'inscription est ferme et définitive». You may change the date once, free of charge, up to 7 days before the test, by email to exams@afmoncton.ca; the registration is in your name and cannot be moved to another test. Your test-day notice arrives at the latest a week before. Allow 20 days between two attempts.

Whichever centre you choose, the results certificate is issued by France Éducation international and accepted by IRCC like any other. Nearby: Halifax ($400; 8 December full on 26 October). Every province is on our list of centres in Canada.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in New Brunswick?

At the Alliance française de Moncton (236 Rue St Georges) or at the CCNB campuses in Edmundston (35, rue du 15-Août) and Bathurst (725, rue du Collège), all approved by France Éducation international and on computer.

When is the next TCF Canada in New Brunswick?

On 26 October 2026: CCNB sessions every weekend from 31 October to 20 December in Edmundston and Bathurst, most with seats left, and ten dates in Moncton from 1 December 2026 to 9 January 2027.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Moncton or Edmundston?

$390 at the Alliance française de Moncton and at the CCNB (26 October 2026).

Can I take the TCF Canada in Edmundston?

Yes: the CCNB's Edmundston campus holds it on Saturdays and Sundays at 8:45 am; on 26 October 2026, every date to 20 December had seats except 1 November.

Does a TCF Canada taken in New Brunswick count for Express Entry?

Yes: the results certificate is issued by France Éducation international, whichever approved centre you test at, and is valid for two years.
