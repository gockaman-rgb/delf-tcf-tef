DELF exam dates 2027 in Turkey: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 4 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Turkey (delf sınav tarihleri 2027) had not been published on 26 October 2026. In 2026 the Institut français de Turquie held five adult sessions — February, March, June, October and November; the last, B2 on 16 November and C1 on 17 November, closed its registration on 9 October. The written papers fall on the same day nationwide, and you register with the Institut français in Istanbul, Ankara or Izmir: ifturquie.org.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026).
  • 5 adult sessions in 2026: February (B2, C1), March, June, October (all levels) and November (B2, C1).
  • Written papers on the same day nationwide; the speaking test is scheduled for you within the month.
  • Fees 2026: A1 and A2 4,550 TL · B1 and B2 5,850 TL · C1 and C2 10,050 TL; reduced rate for students.
  • Results on the Institut’s website 15 days after the end of the session.
  • 4 centres: Istanbul, Ankara, Izmir (Institut français) and Bursa (Alliance française).

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

In Turkey the dates and times of the written papers are the same across the country and set in the annual calendar. The speaking test is scheduled automatically within the session month — you cannot choose the day or time. The 2027 calendar had not been published on 26 October 2026.

How to register

Register with the Institut français online; places are limited. Istanbul’s DELF-DALF page links to the registration form for Istanbul, and Ankara and Izmir have their own exam pages. You can enrol directly at any level, without proof of a previous level — choosing the right one is your responsibility. With a valid reason (a medical certificate), you can ask within two working days for a refund or a transfer to the next session.

Adults, juniors and children

Adults sit the DELF-DALF tout public; teenagers can sit the DELF junior and children the DELF Prim, which have the same value as the adult diplomas, with wording and topics adapted to their age. According to the Institut français, the DELF-DALF is recognised in Turkey by public administrations, some banks, Turkish Airlines and French-Turkish companies, and the DELF B2 exempts you from the language test at universities in French-speaking countries.

Results and diplomas

Results are published on the Institut français website 15 days after the end of the session (the June 2026 results are online as a PDF). The pass certificate is e-mailed 15 days after the results; the diploma is collected in person, with ID, from the exam office.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Turkey as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Turkey (delf sınav tarihleri 2027)?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026, the Institut français de Turquie held adult sessions in February (B2, C1), March, June, October (all levels) and November (B2, C1); the 2027 calendar will appear on ifturquie.org.

When is the next DELF exam in Turkey?

The DELF B2 on 16 November 2026 and the DALF C1 on 17 November 2026; registration closed on 9 October 2026. No later 2026 adult session was published.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Turkey?

5,850 TL at the full 2026 rate, 4,050 TL at the reduced rate for students and Institut français learners. A1 and A2 cost 4,550 TL, C1 and C2 10,050 TL.

Can adults take the DELF in Turkey?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public is the adult version. Teenagers can take the DELF junior and children the DELF Prim, which have the same value.

How do I book the DELF in Turkey?

Through the Institut français de Turquie’s online registration form for your city (Istanbul, Ankara or Izmir) during the registration window; the Alliance française de Bursa is also an approved centre.
