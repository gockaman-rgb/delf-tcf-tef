DELF exam dates 2027 in Vietnam: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Vietnam were not yet published on 26 October 2026 — but the November 2026 session is open: register from 19 to 30 October for written papers on 14–15 and 21–22 November (B2 on 22 November). You register at an approved centre — the Institut français in Hanoi, Hue or Da Nang, a university or a language centre — with the form, a copy of your ID or passport and the fee: ifv.vn.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026).
  • November 2026: registration 19–30 October; A1 and C1 on 14 November, B1 on 15, A2 and C2 on 21, B2 on 22.
  • 5 adult sessions in 2026: March, May, July, September and November — not every centre holds each one.
  • Fees: A1 1,500,000 VND · A2 1,700,000 · B1 2,100,000 · B2 2,900,000 · C1 3,100,000 · C2 3,300,000.
  • Late registration: +300,000 VND after the deadline.
  • Centres in Hanoi, Hue, Da Nang, Ho Chi Minh City, Can Tho, Nha Trang, Vinh, Da Lat, Ha Long and more.

How to register

Go to one of the exam centres or registration points with the registration form, a copy of your ID card or passport, and the fee. For the sessions held by the Institut français in Hanoi, Hue and Da Nang, the Institut publishes a step-by-step registration guide on ifv.vn. Registering after the deadline costs 300,000 VND more.

Adults, juniors and children

Adults sit the DELF-DALF tout public. Teenagers aged 12 to 18 can sit the DELF junior (March and September at most centres) and children the DELF Prim (May and November): the diplomas are the same and valid for life. Results are published on ifv.vn.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Vietnam as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Vietnam?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026 the Institut français du Vietnam held five adult sessions — March, May, July, September and November — each over two weekends; the 2027 calendar will appear on ifv.vn.

When is the next DELF B2 exam in Vietnam?

Sunday 22 November 2026, with registration from 19 to 30 October 2026. A1 and C1 are on 14 November, B1 on 15 November, A2 and C2 on 21 November.

How much does the DELF cost in Vietnam?

In 2026: A1 1,500,000 VND, A2 1,700,000, B1 2,100,000, B2 2,900,000, DALF C1 3,100,000 and C2 3,300,000 VND, plus 300,000 VND for a late registration.

Can adults take the DELF in Vietnam?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public is the adult version; the DELF junior is for 12- to 18-year-olds and the DELF Prim for children.

How do I book the DELF in Vietnam?

At an approved centre or registration point, with the form, a copy of your ID card or passport and the fee, during the registration window — for November 2026, 19 to 30 October.
