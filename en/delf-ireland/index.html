DELF exam dates 2027 in Ireland: the calendar, fees and the 5 Alliances françaises

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

DELF exam dates 2027 in Ireland: the 2027 calendar published by the Alliance française de Limerick puts the DELF B2 on 18 February, 22 April, 24 June, 23 September and 25 November 2027, with the DALF C1 and C2 the next day in Dublin; registration for February runs from 11 November 2026 to 11 January 2027. Before that, Dublin holds its session from 24 to 27 November 2026, B2 on the 26th. The DELF and DALF are held at Ireland’s 5 Alliances françaises — Dublin, Cork, Kilkenny, Limerick, Waterford-Wexford —, managed by the Alliance in Dublin.

Key facts
  • 5 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Alliances françaises in Dublin, Cork, Kilkenny, Limerick and Waterford-Wexford.
  • 2027 (Limerick): A1 and A2 on Tuesday, B1 on Wednesday, B2 on Thursday, C1 and C2 on Friday — the weeks of 16 February, 20 April, 22 June, 21 September and 23 November.
  • The DALF C1 and C2 are taken in Dublin, the Alliance in Limerick points out.
  • November 2026 in Dublin: A1 and A2 on the 24th, B1 on the 25th, B2 on the 26th, C1 and C2 on 27 November.
  • Fees: B2 €145 in Dublin, €170 in Limerick (2027 fees); DALF C1 €195 and €220.
  • Results about one month after the exam; diploma 3 to 4 months after (Limerick).

The individual speaking test may be on a different day from the written papers. The Alliance française de Dublin, the managing centre, showed three sessions for 2026 — February, June and November —, the last from 24 to 27 November 2026; it had not put its 2027 dates online on 26 October. The DELF junior and DELF scolaire have their own sessions (January, March and May 2027 in Limerick), the DELF Prim in May and June.

In Limerick, the reduced fee (10% off) is for the Alliance’s students and for learners from partner organisations — schools, higher-education institutions, public administrations. The DELF junior there costs €95 to €145, the DELF scolaire €50 to €75, the DELF Prim €45 to €65.

Registration

In Limerick, you download the form for your exam, email it to the Alliance, then pay; for under-18s, a parent signs it. In Dublin, the test-day notice arrives one week before the exam. The diplomas are independent: you register directly for the level you want, and you need 50 points out of 100 to pass.

The 5 DELF-DALF test centres in Ireland

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Gestion Centrale DELF/DALF (Dublin) · 1 Kildare Street, Dublin 2 Dublin · alliance-francaise.ie. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Ireland?

According to the 2027 calendar of the Alliance française de Limerick, the DELF B2 is on 18 February, 22 April, 24 June, 23 September and 25 November 2027, with the DALF C1 and C2 the next day, in Dublin. For February, registration runs from 11 November 2026 to 11 January 2027.

When is the next DELF exam in Dublin?

From 24 to 27 November 2026: A1 and A2 on the 24th, B1 on the 25th, B2 on the 26th, DALF C1 and C2 on 27 November, according to the Alliance française de Dublin’s page.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Ireland?

€145 at the Alliance française de Dublin, according to the fees shown on 26 October 2026; €170 in Limerick in 2027, €153 at the reduced rate.

Where can I take the DALF C1 in Ireland?

In Dublin: the Alliance française de Limerick points out that all DALF C1 and C2 papers are held there. The C1 costs €195 in Dublin.
