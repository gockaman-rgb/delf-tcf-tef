TCF Canada in Italy: the test centres that offer it, fees and dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Italy, 14 centres are approved for the TCF; on 26 October 2026, seven advertised the TCF Canada on their websites: the Alliances françaises in Turin (€220), Genoa (€210), Venice (€250), Bari (€220), Aosta and Latina, and the Institut français in Milan (€240). Next sessions: Latina on 13 November, Turin on 1 December, Milan on 10 December, Venice on 16 December 2026, Genoa on 16 January 2027. The Centre Saint-Louis in Rome only offers the tout public, IRN and Québec versions.

Key facts
  • 14 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), 7 advertising the TCF Canada: Turin, Genoa, Venice, Bari, Aosta, Latina and the Institut français in Milan.
  • TCF Canada: €210 in Genoa, €220 in Turin and Bari, €240 in Milan, €250 in Venice; Aosta and Latina don’t publish their fee.
  • Sessions: Latina 13 Nov and 11 Dec; Turin 1 Dec (deadline 6 Nov); Milan 10 Dec (registration until 30 Oct); Venice 16 Dec 2026 (deadline 13 Nov); Genoa 16 Jan 2027 (deadline 21 Dec).
  • In 2027: Milan 9 Mar; Turin 16 Mar and 6 Jul; Genoa 6 Mar, 8 May and 10 Jul.
  • No TCF Canada at the Centre Saint-Louis in Rome (tout public, IRN, Québec) or at the Alliance in Padua (tout public only).
  • TEF Canada, the other test IRCC accepts: sessions published by the Alliance française in Vicenza on 26 October 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Italy?

On 26 October 2026, seven approved centres advertised it: the Alliances françaises in Turin, Genoa, Venice, Bari, Aosta and Latina, and the Institut français in Milan. The Centre Saint-Louis in Rome does not offer it.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Italy?

€210 in Genoa, €220 in Turin and Bari, €240 at the Institut français in Milan, €250 in Venice. Aosta and Latina didn’t publish their fee.

Can I take the TCF Canada in Rome?

Not at the Centre Saint-Louis, the Institut français in Rome: it offers the TCF tout public, the TCF IRN and the TCF Québec. The nearest centre that advertises it is the Alliance française Sud Latium, in Latina.

When is the next TCF Canada in Milan?

On 10 December 2026, with registration from 5 to 30 October, then on 9 March 2027 (registration from 11 to 31 January), at €240.

Is the TCF computer-based in Italy?

It depends on the centre: Turin and Latina run it on computer; Genoa, for now, only on paper.
