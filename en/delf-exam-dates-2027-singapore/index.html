DELF exam dates 2027 in Singapore: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 3 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Singapore were not yet published on 26 October 2026. The next adult session runs from 23 to 27 November 2026 — A1 on Monday 23, B2 on Thursday 26, the DALF C1 and C2 on Friday 27 — but its registration closed on 18 October. The Alliance Française de Singapour, the only approved centre, held three sessions in 2026 (March, May and November); you book online or at its desk: alliancefrancaise.org.sg.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026); in 2026, registration for March ran from 5 January to 15 February.
  • November 2026: A1 Mon 23 · A2 Tue 24 · B1 Wed 25 · B2 Thu 26 · C1 and C2 Fri 27 November; registration closed on 18 October.
  • 3 adult sessions in 2026: 16–20 March, 18–22 May and 23–27 November.
  • Fees 2026 (GST included): A1 S$220 · A2 S$270 · B1 S$330 · B2 S$380 · C1 and C2 S$450; lower rates for AF members.
  • DELF Junior (13–17) the week before: 16–19 November 2026; DELF Prim (8–12) on 20 November.
  • One centre: Alliance Française de Singapour, 1 Sarkies Road.

How to register

Register online — each level of the tout public session is a separate entry on the Alliance’s DELF Tout public page, paid online — or at the registration desk in the lobby of 1 Sarkies Road, during the registration window. The Alliance’s DELF/DALF Terms and Conditions 2026 apply. Teachers who want to register several students at once write to the Alliance’s academic department. Calendar, fees and registration links: alliancefrancaise.org.sg.

Adults, juniors and children

The Alliance Française de Singapour, the only official centre in Singapore, offers three versions: the DELF Prim for children aged 8 to 12, the DELF Junior for teenagers aged 13 to 17, and the DELF and DALF tout public for adults. The diplomas are issued by the French Ministry of Education and are valid for life.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Singapore as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Singapore?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026 the Alliance Française de Singapour held three adult sessions — 16–20 March, 18–22 May and 23–27 November — and registration for the March session ran from 5 January to 15 February 2026.

When is the next DELF exam in Singapore?

The November 2026 session: A1 on 23 November, A2 on 24, B1 on 25, B2 on 26, DALF C1 and C2 on 27 November 2026. Registration closed on 18 October 2026.

How much is the DELF B2 in Singapore?

S$380 including GST, or S$270 for Alliance Française members (2026 fees). A1 costs S$220, A2 S$270, B1 S$330, DALF C1 and C2 S$450.

Can adults take the DELF in Singapore?

Yes: the DELF and DALF tout public are for adults. Teenagers aged 13 to 17 can take the DELF Junior and children aged 8 to 12 the DELF Prim.

How do I book the DELF in Singapore?

Online on the Alliance Française de Singapour’s website, level by level, or at its registration desk at 1 Sarkies Road, during the registration window.
