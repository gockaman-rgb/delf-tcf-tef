TCF Canada in Winnipeg: the Alliance Française du Manitoba, the fee and the dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

In Winnipeg, the TCF Canada is held by the Alliance Française du Manitoba, 934 Corydon Avenue — the only approved TCF centre in Manitoba — for $400. On 26 October 2026, its TCF page listed three «new dates», 9, 11 and 14 December, all sold out, and nothing for 2027. Registration takes four steps — the form and your ID, the payment, then a date confirmed by email — and the fee is neither refunded nor deferred.

Key facts
  • 1 approved centre in Manitoba: the Alliance Française du Manitoba, 934 Corydon Avenue.
  • TCF Canada $400; optional PrepMyFuture online preparation, $80.
  • ⚠️ 26 October 2026: 9, 11 and 14 December sold out; no other date listed.
  • Reading, listening and writing on computer; speaking face to face, 12 minutes; the exam day runs from 9:50 am to 6 pm.
  • 30 days between two attempts; results within 4 weeks; «No refund or deferment is possible».

Booking the TCF Canada in Winnipeg

The Alliance's TCF page sets out four steps: fill in the TCF form with a piece of ID (passport, residence permit or national identity card), pay — online or by phone — and wait for the email that confirms your date. «The registration is not complete until payment has been received.» The exam day runs from 9:50 am to 6 pm; results arrive by email from France Éducation international within four weeks, and «There is absolutely no way to speed up the process». Cancellations «for climatic or personal reasons are not possible». For new dates, watch the page or write to info@afmanitoba.ca.

On 26 October 2026, the nearest centres were sold out too — Calgary and Edmonton ($400). Open dates were in New Brunswick ($390) and Ottawa ($400).
