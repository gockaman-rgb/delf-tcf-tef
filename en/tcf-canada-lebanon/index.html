TCF Canada in Lebanon: the six Institut français centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 13 October 2026 · 5 min read

اقرأ بالعربية

In Lebanon, the TCF is run by the Institut français du Liban, and France Éducation international lists 6 approved centres: Beirut, Deir El Kamar, Jounieh, Saida, Tripoli and Zahlé. On 13 October 2026, each of their exam pages named the TCF Canada among the versions offered, “as part of the Canadian immigration process”. Session dates are only in PDF calendars, and no fee is published as text; results arrive about six weeks after the test.

Key facts
  • 6 approved centres (FEI list of 13 October 2026), all Institut français du Liban; computer sessions in Beirut, Deir El Kamar and Tripoli.
  • The TCF Canada is listed on all six exam pages, with the TCF tout public, the TCF Québec and the TCF IRN.
  • Results in about 6 weeks; the certificate is collected in person at the Institut.
  • Dates: in PDF calendars only (Beirut, Jounieh and Saida for 2026; Zahlé’s is the 2024 one; none linked for Deir El Kamar and Tripoli); no fee published as text.
  • The results certificate is valid for two years for IRCC.

What the Institut français du Liban publishes

Each of the six centres has its own exam page on institutfrancais-liban.com, with the same TCF text: the TCF tout public, the TCF Québec, the TCF Canada — “as part of the Canadian immigration process” — and the TCF IRN. The results are sent “about 6 weeks after the exams”, and the certificate is collected in person at the Institut.

The calendars, however, are PDFs: Beirut, Jounieh and the South (Saida) link a 2026 TCF calendar, Zahlé still links its 2024 one, and Deir El Kamar and Tripoli link none. The pages don’t say how to register or by when, and publish no fee as text — so contact your centre (Beirut gives 01 420 272/3 on its page) before planning your application. If you prefer the other test IRCC accepts, see TCF vs TEF Canada.

The “TCF Canada” badges come from our check of each centre’s website on 13 October 2026. An organisation that is not on this list is not approved.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Lebanon?

At the Institut français du Liban, in any of its six approved centres: Beirut, Deir El Kamar, Jounieh, Saida, Tripoli and Zahlé. All six exam pages listed the TCF Canada on 13 October 2026.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Lebanon?

The Institut français du Liban doesn’t publish its TCF fees as text on its exam pages; ask your centre (Beirut: 01 420 272/3).

Is the TCF Canada computer-based in Lebanon?

FEI’s list shows computer sessions in Beirut, Deir El Kamar and Tripoli; the other three centres don’t declare that option.

How long do results take?

About six weeks after the test; the certificate is collected in person at the Institut français du Liban.
