TCF Canada in Seattle: not offered — the TEF Canada instead, and where to take the TCF

By Augusto Grone · Updated October 26, 2026 · 5 min read

If you are looking for the TCF Canada in Seattle, the answer is no: the Alliance française de Seattle, approved by France Éducation international for the TCF, shows no TCF on its website and offers the TEF Canada instead — the other French test IRCC accepts — for $365. On October 26, 2026, every TEF session from October 30 to December 21 was sold out, and the January–March 2027 dates were due «late November 2026». The other approved center in the area, the French American School of Puget Sound on Mercer Island, publishes no TCF information.

Key facts
  • Approved TCF centers in the Seattle area: the Alliance française (4649 Sunnyside Ave N) and the French American School of Puget Sound (Mercer Island) — neither advertises the TCF Canada.
  • TEF Canada $365, four modules; reading, listening and writing on computer, speaking with an examiner.
  • ⚠️ October 26, 2026: every TEF Canada session from October 30 to December 21 sold out; «January to March 2027 dates will be published late November 2026».
  • 20 days between two TEF registrations; results by email about 1 to 10 days after the exam.
  • Nearest TCF Canada on October 26: Vancouver ($390, December sold out), San Francisco ($460, waitlist), Pluma Academy in Denver ($435).

TEF Canada in Seattle, or the TCF Canada elsewhere

For Express Entry or Canadian citizenship, IRCC accepts either the TCF Canada or the TEF Canada; the score tables differ, but both convert to CLB levels (TCF vs TEF Canada). In Seattle, only the TEF Canada is on offer, at the Alliance française: $365 for the four modules, with reading, listening and writing on computer and the speaking test with an examiner. On October 26, 2026, its sessions from October 30 to December 21 were all sold out; the January–March 2027 dates were to be published in late November, so watch the page then.

If you have prepared for the TCF Canada specifically, the nearest centers on October 26 were across the border in Vancouver ($390, December sold out) and in San Francisco ($460, waitlist); Pluma Academy in Denver ($435) listed sessions on most weekdays to March 2027.
