The TCF Canada at the Institut français d’Égypte

The Institut français d’Égypte publishes its sessions for all its centres on one page: on 13 October 2026, the TCF Canada had its own section — next to the TCF tout public, the TCF IRN, the TCF Québec and the e-TEF — announcing the next session in December 2026 and stating that “registration is currently closed”. The exact date and the fee are in the institute’s PDF calendar (“Dates et tarifs du TCF”), which downloads as a file rather than opening, so we have not read it.

Which centre runs it? The Mounira centre in Cairo has published TCF Canada results for past sessions; the TCF Canada results sections of Alexandria and Heliopolis were empty on 13 October, so we have not confirmed that they hold the TCF Canada. You register with an online form for the version you want, then send a copy of your passport and pay; your exam invitation arrives by email. Results come “within about 4 weeks” of the test, and you must wait 30 days between two TCF attempts. The institute also offers the TEF Canada (e-TEF), with registration closed on 13 October.

The 3 approved TCF centres in Egypt

How to read this list. It reproduces the official list of test centres approved by France Éducation international (FEI), read on 13 October 2026: name, address, phone, generic email and website as FEI publishes them; where the listed website no longer works or has moved, we give the one we opened that day. “Computer-based”: FEI lists the centre as offering computer sessions; “paper-based”: FEI lists no computer sessions. The “TCF Canada” badges come from our check of each centre’s website on 13 October 2026. An organisation that is not on this list is not approved.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Egypt?

At the Institut français d’Égypte. FEI lists three approved TCF centres (Cairo – Mounira, Cairo – Heliopolis, Alexandria); Mounira has published TCF Canada results, while the TCF Canada results sections of the other two were empty on 13 October 2026.

When is the next TCF Canada session in Egypt?

December 2026, according to the Institut français d’Égypte’s website on 13 October 2026; registration was closed that day. The exact date is in its PDF calendar.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Egypt?

The Institut français d’Égypte publishes its TCF fees in a PDF calendar (“Dates et tarifs du TCF”), not on a web page; we could not read it, so check it on ifegypte.com before registering.

How do I register?

With the online form for the TCF Canada, a copy of your passport and the payment; your exam invitation is then sent by email.

How long do results take?

About four weeks after the test, according to the institute’s FAQ. The results certificate is valid for two years for IRCC.
