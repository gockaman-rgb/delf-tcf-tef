TCF Canada in Egypt: the Institut français centres and the next session

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

اقرأ بالعربية

In Egypt, France Éducation international lists 3 approved TCF centres, all part of the Institut français d’Égypte: Cairo (Mounira and Heliopolis) and Alexandria. Its 2026 TCF calendar, read on 26 October 2026, gives the next TCF Canada at Mounira on Tuesday 8 December 2026, with registration from 2 to 12 November. It gives no fee: the institute’s last published fee list (2024) set the TCF Canada at the equivalent of €250, paid in Egyptian pounds at the exchange rate of the month you pay. Results come in about four weeks.

The Institut français d’Égypte publishes its sessions for all its centres on one page, with a link to its 2026 TCF calendar (“Dates et tarifs du TCF”, PDF). On 26 October 2026 the page still said that TCF Canada registration was closed and that the next session was in December; the calendar gives the date — Tuesday 8 December 2026, at the Mounira centre in Cairo — and the registration period, 2 to 12 November 2026. Despite its title, it gives no fee.

Which centre runs it? The Mounira centre in Cairo has published TCF Canada results for past sessions; the TCF Canada results sections of Alexandria and Heliopolis were empty on 13 October, so we have not confirmed that they hold the TCF Canada. You register with an online form for the version you want, then send a copy of your passport and pay; your exam invitation arrives by email. Results come “within about 4 weeks” of the test, and you must wait 30 days between two TCF attempts. The institute also offers the e-TEF Canada: its own 2026 calendar lists 10 February, 30 April and 22 October, yet on 26 October 2026 the TEF centre map of Le français des affaires (CCI Paris) still showed TEF Canada sessions for the Cairo institute — check the next date with the institute.

The fee in Egyptian pounds

The Institut français d’Égypte has not published its 2026 TCF fees: the 2026 calendar lists dates only, and no web page gives a price. The last fee list it published, in its 2024 TCF flyer, set the TCF Canada at the equivalent of €250 (the TCF tout public at €200, the TCF IRN at €175), paid in Egyptian pounds at the exchange rate of the month of payment, rounded up. So the amount in pounds is not fixed: it follows the euro rate of the month you pay, and the 2026 base may differ from 2024’s. Ask the institute for the exact amount when registration opens on 2 November, before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Egypt?

At the Institut français d’Égypte. FEI lists three approved TCF centres (Cairo – Mounira, Cairo – Heliopolis, Alexandria); Mounira has published TCF Canada results, while the TCF Canada results sections of the other two were empty on 13 October 2026.

When is the next TCF Canada session in Egypt?

Tuesday 8 December 2026, at the Mounira centre in Cairo; registration runs from 2 to 12 November 2026 (the Institut français d’Égypte’s 2026 TCF calendar, read on 26 October 2026).

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Egypt, in Egyptian pounds?

The 2026 fee is not published: the 2026 calendar lists dates only. The institute’s last fee list (2024) set the TCF Canada at the equivalent of €250, paid in Egyptian pounds at the exchange rate of the month of payment, rounded up — so the amount in pounds changes from month to month. Ask the institute before paying.

How do I register?

With the online form for the TCF Canada, a copy of your passport and the payment; your exam invitation is then sent by email.

How long do results take?

About four weeks after the test, according to the institute’s FAQ. The results certificate is valid for two years for IRCC.
