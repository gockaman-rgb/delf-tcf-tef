TCF Canada in San Diego: the Alliance Française, the fee and the sessions

By Augusto Grone · Updated 18 October 2026 · 5 min read

In San Diego, the TCF Canada is held by the Alliance Française de San Diego (6390 Greenwich Dr, #185) for $450, with the written part on computer. On October 18, 2026, every listed session was closed: October 20 (registration ended October 14), then November 4 and November 10, both sold out — and no December or 2027 date was listed yet. A waitlist is open; Los Angeles had dates every month.

Key facts
  • 1 approved center: the Alliance Française de San Diego, 6390 Greenwich Dr, #185 (UTC area).
  • TCF Canada $450; no member rate shown.
  • «The written part is a computer-based format»; about 3 hours for the written part and 15 minutes for the oral, in person only.

Getting a seat in San Diego

The Alliance publishes its sessions in a table on its TCF Canada page, with a registration window for each: the November 4 and 10 sessions opened on July 13, 2026 and were sold out by mid-October. Written test and oral take place on the same day — for November, written 8:40 am to 12 pm, oral 12:30 to 2 pm — at the Alliance, «in person only». If a session is full, join the waitlist from the page, and watch for the next sessions, not yet published on October 18. In the meantime, the Alliance Française de Los Angeles listed November 20, December 18 and monthly 2027 dates; its official site is afsandiego.org — afsd.org is a parked page, not the Alliance.
