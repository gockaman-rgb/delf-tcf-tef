    TCF Canada, TCF IRN, DELF or TEF: which one?

    • TCF Canada — Canadian immigrationAccepted by IRCC, like the TEF Canada; results are valid for two years. Use the “TCF” filter, then check on the centre’s website that it offers the Canada version.
    • TCF Québec — immigration to QuebecTests of your choice; same TCF centres, same check to make.
    • TCF IRN — French citizenship, resident cardThe test for French procedures: B2 is required for citizenship since 2026, B1 for the resident card.
    • DELF and DALF — a diplomaDiplomas valid for life, taken level by level; adults take the DELF tout public. Use the “DELF-DALF” filter.
    • TEF Canada, TEF IRN — the other family of testsRun by the Le français des affaires network (CCI Paris Île-de-France): use the “TEF” filter. TCF vs TEF Canada.
    • French civics test — residence permits, citizenshipRequired since 2026 alongside the French test; the “Civics test” filter shows the centres approved by FEI in France.

    What the official list doesn’t tell you

    The official lists say that a centre is approved. They don’t say which version of the TCF it runs (Canada, Québec, IRN), nor its dates or fees: there is no national fee, and two centres in the same city can have unrelated sessions and prices. For the TEF, the CCI map shows the sessions open on the day it was read, not the tests a centre is approved for. What we checked centre by centre is in our country and city guides, the TCF Canada centres in Canada and TCF Canada test dates. An organisation that is not on these lists is not approved.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a TCF Canada test centre near me?

    Type your city or tap “Near me” with the TCF filter: the nearest approved TCF centres appear, with the distance. France Éducation international’s list doesn’t say which ones offer the TCF Canada: check the centre’s website — our country and city guides checked it for the main centres. The TEF Canada, which IRCC also accepts, is taken at the centres under the TEF filter.

    Can adults take the DELF?

    Yes: the DELF tout public is the version for adults. The DELF junior and DELF scolaire are for teenagers — in France, the education authority centres (CASNAV) focus on the DELF scolaire. Ask the centre which version it runs before you register.

    How do I book a test date?

    Directly with the centre, usually online — never with France Éducation international or CCI Paris Île-de-France. Each card gives the centre’s website, phone number and email; for the TEF, the “Contact page (CCI)” link opens the centre’s page on the CCI platform.

    How up to date is this list?

    It reproduces the official lists read on October 26, 2026 — France Éducation international for the TCF and DELF-DALF, CCI Paris Île-de-France for the TEF — and the list of September 19, 2026 for the civics test. Centres come and go as approvals change: if in doubt, the official list is the reference.

    Is my location sent anywhere?

    No. “Near me” asks your browser for your position only when you tap the button, and distances are computed in the page: neither your position nor your search leaves your device. The page calls no third-party service.
