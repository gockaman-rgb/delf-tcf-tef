DELF exam dates 2027 in China: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in China were not yet published on 26 October 2026. The last 2026 session starts on 7 November: DALF C1 and C2 on 7–8 November, B2 on 14–15 November, A1, A2 and B1 on 21–22 November (registration closed on 22 September). Mainland China has one national calendar, usually March, June and November, and you register online only, on delf-dalf.neea.edu.cn, the platform of the Ministry of Education’s examination authority.

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

In mainland China the DELF and DALF are run with the Education Examinations Authority of the Ministry of Education (NEEA), in cooperation with France Éducation international: the same exam days in every centre, three sessions a year, “usually in March, June and November”. Each session spreads over three weekends — the DALF first, then the B2, then A1, A2 and B1. On the first day of each weekend the written papers are in the morning and the speaking tests start in the afternoon; the speaking tests continue all the next day. How to register

Registration is online only, on delf-dalf.neea.edu.cn (Chinese), during the window of each session:

  1. create an account on NEEA’s candidate site and log in;
  2. choose the session, the centre and the level, and fill in your details exactly as they should appear on the diploma — surname, first name, nationality, mother tongue, e-mail;
  3. upload a recent colour photo (white or blue background, 3.5 × 4.9 cm, JPG or PNG under 30 KB): it is printed on the admission ticket and used to check your identity;
  4. pay online (Alipay, China Merchants Bank or Bank of China); a confirmation e-mail follows.

The rules NEEA publishes: you may register for only one level at a time; places depend on each centre’s capacity and go until it is full; once registered you cannot change the date, level or centre or cancel, and a candidate who does not sit is not refunded. Mainland candidates register with their resident identity card and bring the same document on the day; arrive at least 30 minutes before the start — late candidates cannot sit any paper.

Adults, juniors and children

Adults sit the DELF-DALF tout public. The DELF Junior is for candidates aged 12 to 17 and the DELF Prim for children; the junior diploma is the same as the adult one. Under the French Ministry of Education’s rules, the exam is open to candidates who are not French nationals, and to French nationals born in a non-French-speaking country who have not studied in French secondary or higher education.

Results and diplomas

Results are posted on the registration site about a month after the last level of a session. You pass with 50/100. Within a month of the results you can collect your score sheet or provisional certificate from your centre or local Alliance française, and ask to see your paper (only simple addition errors are corrected); the diploma arrives two to three months after the exam.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in China as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in China?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. Mainland China holds three adult sessions a year, usually in March, June and November; in 2026, registration for the March session ran from 6 to 19 January. The 2027 dates will be announced on delf-dalf.neea.edu.cn.

When is the next DELF exam in China?

DALF C1 and C2 on 7–8 November 2026, DELF B2 on 14–15 November and DELF A1, A2 and B1 on 21–22 November 2026. Registration for that session closed on 22 September 2026.

How much does the DELF cost in China?

The fees are national: 1,500 yuan for the DELF A1 and A2 (and the DELF Prim), 2,000 yuan for the B1 and B2, 2,500 yuan for the DALF C1 and C2, paid online when you register.

Can I register for two DELF levels at once in China?

No. NEEA allows one level at a time, and once registered you cannot change the level, date or centre, cancel, or be refunded if you do not sit.

Can adults take the DELF in China?

Yes: the DELF-DALF tout public is the adult version. The DELF Junior is for 12- to 17-year-olds and leads to the same diploma; children sit the DELF Prim.

How do I book the DELF in China?

Online only, on delf-dalf.neea.edu.cn: create an account, choose the session, centre and level, upload your photo and pay with Alipay, China Merchants Bank or Bank of China during the registration window.
