TCF Canada in Kolkata: the Alliance Française du Bengale, the fees and the rules

By Augusto Grone · Updated 17 October 2026 · 5 min read

In Kolkata, the TCF is held by the Alliance Française du Bengale, which offers the TCF Canada, the TCF Québec, the TCF tout public and the TCF IRN, on computer. On 8 October 2026 the TCF Canada cost ₹26,000 including GST; dates are on an external booking calendar, and you must register at least 15 days before the test.

Key facts
  • Alliance Française du Bengale: TCF Canada, Québec, tout public and IRN, computer-based.
  • Fees: TCF Canada ₹26,000 incl. GST · TCF Québec ₹6,500 per test · TCF tout public ₹13,000 (+ ₹6,500 per optional test).
  • Dates on an external booking calendar; register at least 15 days before the test.
  • For sessions from January 2027: one postponement of up to 3 months for 50% of the fee (₹13,000).
  • At least 20 days between two attempts.

Fees and booking in Kolkata

The Alliance Française du Bengale publishes its fees: the TCF Canada at ₹26,000 including GST, the TCF Québec at ₹6,500 per test — useful if a Quebec programme only asks for the oral tests — and the TCF tout public at ₹13,000 plus ₹6,500 per optional test. You book on an external calendar, with an online form that includes payment, at least 15 days before the test. For sessions from January 2027, one postponement of up to three months is possible for 50% of the fee (₹13,000). Leave at least 20 days between two attempts. Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Kolkata?

At the Alliance Française du Bengale, on computer, for ₹26,000 including GST (8 October 2026).

How much is the TCF Québec in Kolkata?

₹6,500 per test at the Alliance Française du Bengale, so you pay only for the tests your Quebec programme requires.

Can I postpone my TCF in Kolkata?

For sessions from January 2027, once, by up to three months, for 50% of the fee (₹13,000).
