DELF exam dates 2027 in Portugal: the national calendar, fees and the 16 centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Portugal (datas exames DELF 2027) were not yet published on 26 October 2026. Portugal follows a national calendar from the Institut français du Portugal: five sessions in 2026, the last on 19 and 20 November (A1, B1 and C1 on the 19th; A2, B2 and C2 on the 20th), with registration until 6 November at seven Alliances françaises. The DELF B2 costs €140, or €126 at the reduced rate.

Key facts
  • 16 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), managed by the language and education cooperation service (Lisbon): nine Alliances françaises, the universities of Aveiro, Porto and the Azores, two French lycées, an international school and the Academia de Línguas da Madeira.
  • November 2026: A1, B1 and C1 on the 19th; A2, B2 and C2 on 20 November; speaking tests from 16 to 27 November; registration until 6 November; results on 15 December.
  • Where, in November: the Alliances françaises in Porto, Guimarães, Coimbra, Faro, Viseu, Setúbal and Leiria.
  • In 2026: February, April, June, September and November; 2027 not published.
  • Fees: A1 €65 · A2 €75 · B1 €110 · B2 €140 · C1 €177 · C2 €190; reduced, €59 to €171.
  • The DELF Prim, DELF junior and DELF escolar have their own calendar.

Not every town opens every session: in February and September, the Alliances in Lisbon, Porto, Guimarães, Coimbra, Faro, Viseu, Setúbal and Leiria and the universities of Porto and Aveiro; in June, the same plus the Academia de Línguas da Madeira; in November, only the Alliances in Porto, Guimarães, Coimbra, Faro, Viseu, Setúbal and Leiria.

The reduced fee applies to candidates enrolled at the centres named in the calendar and to group registrations of ten or more candidates per level. The DELF-DALF tout public is for candidates aged 18 and over; younger candidates take the DELF junior or DELF escolar (primary and secondary pupils) or the DELF Prim.

Registration

You register with one of the centres of your chosen session; the Institut français du Portugal keeps the calendar and publishes, for each diploma, a PDF of dates and fees. After an IT incident, it extended the deadlines for changing or cancelling a registration by 14 days. The diplomas are independent: you register directly for the level you want, and you need 50 points out of 100 to pass.

The 16 DELF-DALF test centres, city by city

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Service de Coopération linguistique et éducative (Lisbon) · Rua Santos-o-Velho,, 1200-811 Lisbonne · ifp-lisboa.com. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Portugal?

Not yet published: on 26 October 2026, the Institut français du Portugal only showed its 2026 calendar of five sessions (February, April, June, September, November).

When is the next DELF exam in Portugal?

On 19 and 20 November 2026: A1, B1 and DALF C1 on the 19th, A2, B2 and C2 on the 20th, at the Alliances françaises in Porto, Guimarães, Coimbra, Faro, Viseu, Setúbal and Leiria. Registration until 6 November, results on 15 December 2026.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Portugal?

€140, or €126 at the reduced rate (2026 fees of the Institut français du Portugal). The DALF C1 costs €177 and C2 €190.

Can I take the DELF in Lisbon in November?

No: in November 2026, the national calendar only holds the exam at the Alliances in Porto, Guimarães, Coimbra, Faro, Viseu, Setúbal and Leiria. The Alliance française in Lisbon hosts the February, June and September sessions.
