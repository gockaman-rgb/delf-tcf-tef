delf-tcf-tef.fr is an independent site documenting the French exams — DELF, DALF, TCF, TEF — from official sources, with the date of every reading. It is backed by the exam-prep app “TCF DELF TEF: Tests 2026”. This page gathers what a journalist, teacher or blogger can cite, and how to reach us.

Citable facts (dated readings, sources on every page)

  • 1,825 exam centres in 176 countries, from the lists of France Éducation international (TCF, DELF-DALF) and the CCI Paris Île-de-France (TEF) read on 26 October 2026, searchable by city in the centre finder (French, English, Spanish, Arabic).
  • The national DELF-DALF 2027 calendar: ten sessions a year, never in April or September, with registration windows — in English, in French, in Spanish.
  • TCF Canada fees country by country, centre by centre, each with its reading date: in English, in French, in Arabic.
  • Where to take the TCF Canada in Canada by province, the United States, the United Kingdom and India.
  • The level required for French citizenship (B2 since 2026) — the guide.
  • Re-marking of the TCF writing and speaking papers is suspended by France Éducation international for sessions from 1 September 2026, with a return announced for autumn 2027.

The app

“TCF DELF TEF: Tests 2026” (iOS; Android in closed testing in October 2026) offers mock exams in the official format of fifteen exams — TCF Canada, Québec, IRN, tout public and DAP, DELF A1 to B2, DALF C1 and C2, TEF Canada, TEFAQ and TEF IRN — scored like the real test, with AI feedback on writing and speaking. Interface in French, English, Spanish and Arabic. App Store listing.

Who

The site and the app are published by Augusto Grone, an independent publisher. The site is neither official nor affiliated with France Éducation international or the CCI Paris Île-de-France; its editorial method (primary sources, cross-checking, no unsourced figure) is public.

Citation terms

The site's facts and tables may be cited and reproduced freely with the credit “delf-tcf-tef.fr”, the reading date shown on the page and a link to it. We state our own limits: fees, dates and rules change without notice, and the official source prevails.
