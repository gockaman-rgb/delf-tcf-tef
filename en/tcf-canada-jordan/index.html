TCF Canada in Jordan: the Institut français in Amman and its sessions

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

اقرأ بالعربية

Yes: in Jordan, the TCF Canada is held by the Institut français de Jordanie in Amman (Jabal Al-Weibdeh), the only approved TCF centre on France Éducation international’s list of 26 October 2026. Its 2026 calendar has three TCF Canada sessions — 17 February, 15 July and Wednesday 11 November (registration 4 to 6 October, now closed). You register by email; the fee is not published.

Key facts
  • 1 approved TCF centre in Jordan (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Institut français de Jordanie, Amman; FEI lists no computer-based sessions for it.
  • TCF Canada 2026: 17 February, 15 July and Wednesday 11 November; registration 18–20 January, 7–9 June and 4–6 October.
  • Three TCF Canada sessions a year (and two each of the TCF tout public and the TCF IRN).
  • Registration by email (cours@ifjordan.com), during a three-day window four to five weeks before the test.
  • Fee: not published on the institute’s website on 26 October 2026; no 2027 date yet.

The Institut français de Jordanie publishes its TCF dates in a PDF calendar linked from its TCF page. The same calendar lists the TCF tout public on 17 February and 15 July, and the TCF IRN on 29 April and 11 November. You register by email to cours@ifjordan.com during the registration window — three days, four to five weeks before the test. The website gives no fee and does not say whether the test is on paper or on computer; France Éducation international’s list shows no computer-based sessions for this centre, so ask the institute. On 26 October 2026, no 2027 date had been published. The results certificate is valid for two years.

If the Amman dates don’t suit you

The nearest centres that advertised the TCF Canada when we checked them: Lebanon (six Institut français centres), Egypt (Cairo, Tuesday 8 December 2026), Saudi Arabia (1,950 SAR), Qatar (Doha, 1,300 QAR) and the United Arab Emirates (1,800 AED). France Éducation international also lists TCF centres in Iraq (Baghdad, Erbil) and the Palestinian territories (Ramallah, Bethlehem), but on 26 October 2026 none of their websites that we could open mentioned the TCF Canada — in Erbil, the Institut français offers only the TCF tout public, on paper, in May and October.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take the TCF Canada in Jordan?

Yes, at the Institut français de Jordanie in Amman, which holds three TCF Canada sessions a year. Its 2026 calendar lists 17 February, 15 July and Wednesday 11 November (registration 4 to 6 October).

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Jordan?

The Institut français de Jordanie does not publish the fee on its website (26 October 2026); ask for it by email at cours@ifjordan.com before the registration window opens.

How do I register for the TCF Canada in Amman?

By email to cours@ifjordan.com, during the registration window: three days, four to five weeks before the test.

When is the next TCF Canada in Jordan?

Wednesday 11 November 2026, whose registration closed on 6 October. On 26 October 2026, the institute had not published its 2027 dates.

Is a TCF Canada taken in Jordan accepted by IRCC?

Yes: the results certificate is issued by France Éducation international whichever approved centre you test at, and it is valid for two years.
