DELF exam dates 2027 in Italy: the 2026-2027 calendar, fees and the 26 centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

DELF exam dates 2027 in Italy (date esami DELF 2027): the DELF B2 written paper is on 25 February, 8 April and 16 June 2027, in the calendar published identically by the Institut français in Milan and the Alliance française in Turin. Registration for February runs from 2 November to 4 December 2026; the November 2026 session (10 to 13 November) closed on 2 October. The DELF B2 costs €160, or €144 at the reduced rate, in Milan, Turin and Rome.

Key facts
  • 26 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Instituts français in Milan, Florence, Naples and Palermo, the Centre Saint-Louis in Rome, the Lycée Victor-Hugo in Florence and twenty Alliances françaises.
  • February 2027: C2 on the 18th, C1 on the 19th, A1 and A2 on the 23rd, B1 on the 24th, B2 on 25 February; registration from 2 November to 4 December 2026 (same dates in Naples).
  • April 2027: C1 on the 5th, C2 on the 6th, B1 on the 7th, B2 on the 8th, A1 and A2 on 9 April; registration until 19 February. In Milan, April only opens B2, C1 and C2.
  • June 2027: C2 on the 14th, C1 on the 15th, B2 on the 16th, B1 on the 17th, A1 and A2 on 18 June; registration until 23 April 2027.
  • Fees in Milan, Turin and Rome: A1 €80 · A2 €90 · B1 €125 · B2 €160 · C1 and C2 €195; reduced rate €72 to €176.
  • The calendar covers the 2026-2027 year: the November 2027 session was not published on 26 October 2026.

Speaking tests run from 2 to 30 November 2026, 1 to 26 February, 1 to 30 April and 1 to 30 June 2027. The Alliance in Turin warns that registration closes well ahead of the papers. The Institut français in Naples announces four sessions a year (November, February, March-April, June) with the same February window; the Centre Saint-Louis in Rome gives the same months and warns that the speaking test may come before the written papers. Not every centre opens every level at every session.

Registration, results and diploma

You register with your chosen centre — online at the Institut français in Milan, with the exams office elsewhere — during the session’s window. The diplomas are independent: you register directly for the level you want, and you need 50 points out of 100 to pass.

The DELF scolaire, at a preferential fee (€68 for A1, €135 for B2 in Turin), is organised with schools; the DELF Prim is for children. Centres publish each session’s results on their websites.

The 26 DELF-DALF test centres, city by city

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Coopération éducative (Rome) · Piazza Farnese 67, 00186 Rome. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Italy?

According to the 2026-2027 calendar of the Institut français in Milan and the Alliance française in Turin, the DELF B2 is on 25 February, 8 April and 16 June 2027; registration for February runs from 2 November to 4 December 2026. The November 2027 session was not published on 26 October 2026.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Italy?

€160, or €144 at the reduced rate, in Milan, Turin and Rome. The DALF C1 and DALF C2 cost €195 (€176 at the reduced rate).

Can I still register for November 2026?

No: registration for the November 2026 session (written papers from 10 to 13 November) closed on 2 October. The next one is February 2027, with registration from 2 November to 4 December 2026.

Where can I take the DELF in Rome or Milan?

In Rome, at the Institut français – Centre Saint-Louis (Largo Toniolo); in Milan, at the Institut français (Corso Magenta 63). Both charge the same fees, €160 for B2.

Do I need A1 before B2?

No: the DELF and DALF diplomas are independent, so you register directly for the level you want.
