TCF and DELF in Luxembourg: the Institut national des langues, fees and sessions

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

TCF Canada in Luxembourg: not offered. The country’s two approved TCF centres are the sites of the Institut national des langues Luxembourg (INLL), in Luxembourg City and Esch-sur-Alzette (Belval), and its calendar only lists the TCF tout public (€120 for the compulsory tests) and the TCF IRN (€150), on paper. The 2026 sessions are closed; for Canada, the nearest centres are in Brussels (€268), Aachen and Mainz (€295). The INLL also runs the DELF A2 to B2 and the DALF C1.

Key facts
  • 2 approved TCF centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the INLL on boulevard de la Foire in Luxembourg City and its Belval site in Esch-sur-Alzette.
  • Paper-based TCF tout public: €120 for the compulsory tests, +€55 for writing, +€55 for speaking; TCF IRN: €150.
  • 2026 sessions: TCF IRN on 19 Oct (deadline 14 Sep), TCF tout public on 23 Nov (deadline 12 Oct); 2027 not published.
  • No TCF Canada: the INLL’s 2026 TCF registration form only offers tout public and IRN.
  • The exam only goes ahead with at least 5 candidates per level; otherwise the fee is refunded.
  • DELF-DALF at the INLL: B1 €145, B2 €186, C1 €232 (session from 9 to 11 November 2026, B2 full); A2 €124 in February 2027.

Send the file by email or post: form, ID, proof of payment.

The 2026 tests are held at the Glacis site, boulevard de la Foire. Registration may close before the deadline shown; the test-day notice arrives at least two weeks before the first test. In case of force majeure, the INLL can move the registration to a later session, within 12 months, with proof sent within five days. For the TCF IRN, the form asks for the purpose: citizenship, resident card or multi-year residence permit in France.

Where to take the TCF Canada near Luxembourg?

On 26 October 2026, the INLL did not offer the Canada version. The nearest centres that advertise it: the Alliance française de Bruxelles-Europe (€268, session on 9 December 2026), the Institut culturel franco-allemand in Aachen and the Institut français in Mainz (€295), in Germany. The TEF Canada, the other test IRCC accepts, has one centre in Luxembourg on the map of Le français des affaires — inlingua Luxembourg —, with no session published on 26 October 2026.

The DELF and DALF in Luxembourg

Two centres run the DELF-DALF (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the INLL, the country’s managing body, and the Lycée Michel-Lucius in Luxembourg City. The INLL offers the DELF A2, B1 and B2 and the DALF C1:

Frequently asked questions

Can I take the TCF Canada in Luxembourg?

No, not on 26 October 2026: the Institut national des langues, the country’s only TCF provider, only offers the TCF tout public and the TCF IRN. The nearest centres that advertise the TCF Canada are Brussels (€268), Aachen and Mainz (€295).

How much does the TCF IRN cost in Luxembourg?

€150 at the INLL, on paper. The 2026 session was on 19 October (deadline 14 September); the 2027 ones were not published.

How much does the TCF tout public cost in Luxembourg?

€120 for the compulsory tests at the INLL, plus €55 for writing and €55 for speaking. Session on 23 November 2026, registration closed on 12 October.

Where can I take the DELF B2 in Luxembourg?

At the Institut national des langues, €186; the 9 to 11 November 2026 session had no places left on 26 October. The Lycée Michel-Lucius is also a DELF-DALF centre.
