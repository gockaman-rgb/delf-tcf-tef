TCF Canada in Denver: the Alliance française, Pluma Academy, the fees and the dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated October 26, 2026 · 5 min read

Denver has two approved TCF Canada centers: the Alliance française de Denver, at $360, and Pluma Academy, at $435. On October 26, 2026, the Alliance's three remaining sessions — November 16, November 30 and December 7 — were sold out, with no 2027 date yet, while Pluma Academy listed sessions on most weekdays from October 30 to March 27 and calls candidates back within 24 hours of registering. Both test on computer.

Key facts
  • 2 approved centers: the Alliance française de Denver (7701 E 1st Place) and Pluma Academy (940 Fillmore Street).
  • Alliance française: TCF Canada $360; ⚠️ November 16, November 30 and December 7, 2026 sold out; no 2027 date; dates posted about two months ahead; free waitlist.
  • Pluma Academy: TCF Canada $435 on computer ($70 more on paper); dates listed from October 30, 2026 to March 27, 2027, about twenty a month from January.
  • Waiting period: 30 days at the Alliance, 21 days at Pluma Academy.
  • No refund at either center once registered; the Alliance keeps $50 if it approves an exception.

Booking in Denver

At the Alliance française, you register for a TCF Canada session in its online exam selector and pay; the TCF IRN and tout public are on request by email. Exam dates appear about two months before the test, and a full session has a free waiting list. The name you give must match your passport exactly, and «There is no refund once the test is purchased». At Pluma Academy, you click «Register Now» on its TCF page, read its exam policy, and the center calls you within 24 hours to fix the details; «No refund will be made or credit given once the registration is processed». Pluma tests on computer, or on paper for $70 more, with results in eight to ten days on computer. For the other states, see our USA page.
