TCF Canada in Portugal: test centres, fees and sessions

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Portugal, 6 centres are approved for the TCF. On 26 October 2026, three Alliances françaises showed a TCF Canada fee: Porto and Faro (Algarve), €220, and Viseu, €211. In Faro, the test is taken on computer on any working day, on a date the candidate chooses at least 7 days ahead; elsewhere, no dates are online: contact the Alliance. Lisbon and Coimbra state neither the version nor the fee.

Key facts
  • 6 approved centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Alliances françaises of Lisbon, Porto, Coimbra, Viseu and the Algarve (Faro), and the language institute of Universidade Nova de Lisboa.
  • TCF Canada: €220 in Porto and Faro, €211 in Viseu.
  • Faro: computer-based TCF on demand, any working day except 8 to 21 August and the second half of December, booked at least 7 days ahead.
  • TCF IRN €220 in Faro and Porto; TCF tout public €100 for the compulsory tests in Faro.
  • Lisbon: TCF presented with no version or fee, contact form; Coimbra: calendar and fees on request.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Portugal?

On 26 October 2026, three Alliances françaises showed a TCF Canada fee: Porto (€220), Faro in the Algarve (€220) and Viseu (€211). Lisbon and Coimbra run the TCF without listing the versions online.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Portugal?

€220 in Porto and Faro, €211 in Viseu, for the four tests.

Can I choose my TCF Canada date in Portugal?

In Faro, yes: the Alliance de l’Algarve runs the TCF on computer on any working day, except 8 to 21 August and the second half of December, as long as you book at least 7 days ahead.

Where can I take the TCF in Lisbon?

At the Alliance française in Lisbon, which lists neither version nor fee online: use its contact form. The language institute of Universidade Nova de Lisboa is also approved.
