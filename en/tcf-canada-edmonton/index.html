TCF Canada in Edmonton: the Alliance Française, the fee and how to get a seat

By Augusto Grone · Updated 18 October 2026 · 5 min read

In Edmonton, the TCF Canada is held by the Alliance Française d'Edmonton for $400, at its new annex (100, 11812–121 St NW, Kingsway). On 18 October 2026, every listed session — a morning and an afternoon on each date from 19 October to 20 November — was sold out or closed, and no December or 2027 date was listed. Each session's sales open for about an hour on a single day: to get a seat, you have to be online at that moment.

Getting a seat in Edmonton

The Alliance lists its sessions in a table on its TCF page, each with a written test in the morning (9:20 am) or the afternoon (1:30 pm) and an oral «to be confirmed». Registration is online only, «enable only during registration period. Seats are limited.»: each session's window lasted about an hour, on a single day, and every session up to 20 November was sold out by 18 October. The Alliance warns that if your request concerns TEF or TCF dates, «you will not receive an individual response either by email or by phone»: watch the page for the next windows. If you fail, you may retake the test 20 days later; «as of September 1, 2026, TCF score re-evaluations are no longer available until further notice». Calgary was sold out to December; the other provinces are on our list of centres in Canada.
