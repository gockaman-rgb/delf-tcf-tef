TCF Canada and DELF in Austria: the Institut français in Vienna, fees and dates

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Austria, one centre is approved for the TCF: the Institut français d’Autriche in Vienna. It shows the TCF Canada at €295, the TCF IRN at €225 and the TCF tout public from €135 to €295; its only published TCF session is on 4 December 2026, with registration closing on 4 November. For the DELF and DALF, the Institut’s next session falls on 27, 28 and 29 January 2027; the Institut culturel franco-autrichien in Graz holds its own in May 2027.

Key facts
  • 1 approved TCF centre (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Institut français d’Autriche, Praterstraße 38, Vienna.
  • TCF Canada €295 · TCF IRN €225 · TCF tout public €135 (compulsory tests), €235 with writing or speaking, €295 in full · TCF Québec on request.
  • TCF session: 4 Dec 2026, registration until 4 Nov; photo ID compulsory, photo taken on the test day.
  • No re-marking requests for sessions after 1 August 2026.
  • DELF-DALF in Vienna, January 2027: A2 and B1 on the 27th, C1 and C2 on the 28th, A1 and B2 on 29 January; fees €130 to €220, €104 at the student rate; papers also in Salzburg and Innsbruck.
  • Graz: DELF-DALF tout public in May 2027; DELF scolaire on 9 and 10 March 2027 (deadline 29 January).

According to the Institut, the “compulsory tests + writing” option is the official test for the DAP procedure (prior admission request to a French university). No re-marking requests are accepted for sessions after 1 August 2026. The TEF Canada, the other test IRCC accepts, has no centre in Austria on the map of Le français des affaires read on 26 October 2026.

The DELF and DALF in Austria

Two centres run the DELF and DALF tout public (FEI list of 26 October 2026): the Institut français d’Autriche in Vienna, the country’s managing body, which also holds papers in Salzburg and Innsbruck, and the Institut culturel franco-autrichien in Graz, which handles its own registrations. You can sit the exams from the age of 16.

Speaking-test dates are sent after registration closes, at least one week before the written papers. Fees range from €130 to €220 depending on the level, €104 at the student rate; a registration only counts once the fee has arrived, and is only refunded with a medical certificate. In Graz, the DELF-DALF tout public is in May 2027 (deadline not yet set) and the DELF scolaire on 9 and 10 March 2027, with registration and payment by 29 January 2027.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Austria?

At the Institut français d’Autriche in Vienna (Praterstraße 38): it is the country’s only approved TCF centre, and it shows the TCF Canada at €295.

When is the next TCF in Vienna?

On 4 December 2026, with registration closing on 4 November; it was the only TCF session published on 26 October 2026. The TCF Québec is held on a date set on request.

How much does the TCF IRN cost in Austria?

€225 at the Institut français in Vienna; the TCF tout public costs €135 for the compulsory tests and €295 with both expression tests.

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Austria?

At the Institut français in Vienna: DELF A2 and B1 on 27 January, DALF C1 and C2 on 28 January, DELF A1 and B2 on 29 January 2027. In Graz, the tout public session is in May 2027.

How much does the DELF cost in Austria?

€130 to €220 at the Institut français in Vienna depending on the level, €104 at the student rate; Graz sets its own fees.
