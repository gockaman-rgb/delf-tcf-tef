TCF Canada in Alberta: Calgary, Edmonton and Lethbridge — fees, dates and booking

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

In Alberta, three centres are approved for the TCF by France Éducation international, but only two run the TCF Canada: the Alliance Française de Calgary and the Alliance Française d'Edmonton, both at $400, on computer. On 26 October 2026, every session they listed was sold out — in Calgary, October to December, including all ten December test days; in Edmonton, the fifteen sessions shown, from 26 October to 13 November — and neither had published a 2027 date. The University of Lethbridge's French Language Centre offers only the DELF-DALF.

Key facts
  • 3 approved centres in Alberta (FEI list of 26 October 2026): Calgary, Edmonton, Lethbridge — the TCF Canada in Calgary and Edmonton only.
  • $400 in both cities; in Calgary, «no refund or deferment is possible».
  • ⚠️ 26 October 2026: Calgary sold out for October, November and its ten December dates; Edmonton sold out for every session shown, 26 October to 13 November; no 2027 date in either city.
  • Edmonton sells each session during a window of about an hour on one day; Calgary's 14-seat sessions go «first-come, first-served».
  • Lethbridge: DELF-DALF twice a year, no TCF on its pages.
  • 20 days between two attempts in Calgary and Edmonton.

How to get a seat in Alberta

Neither Alliance takes bookings by phone. Calgary publishes its dates on its TCF registration page — «All 2026 exam dates will be published on this page» — and is «working with our partners in Paris to open additional sessions»: you pay online for a date, then receive a form to upload your ID. Edmonton lists each session with its own sales window, often about an hour on a single day, and on 18 October it warned that requests about TEF or TCF dates get no individual answer: watch the page and be online when the window opens. In both cities, allow 20 days between two attempts.

If you only need a seat, look further east: on 26 October 2026, the TCF Canada had open dates at $390 in New Brunswick — every weekend to 20 December at the CCNB in Edmundston and Bathurst, and ten dates from 1 December in Moncton — and in Ottawa ($400) on 10 November. Winnipeg ($400) and Vancouver ($390) were sold out for December. On the TEF side, the map of TEF centres of Le français des affaires (CCI Paris Île-de-France) showed no TEF Canada session published for Calgary or Edmonton on 26 October. Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Alberta?

At the Alliance Française de Calgary (tests at the Kahanoff Centre, #315, 105 12 Avenue SE) or at the Alliance Française d'Edmonton, both on computer. The University of Lethbridge is approved by FEI but offers only the DELF-DALF.

When is the next TCF Canada in Alberta?

On 26 October 2026, every session listed in Calgary (to 22 December) and in Edmonton (to 13 November) was sold out, and neither centre had published a 2027 date.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Alberta?

$400 in Calgary and in Edmonton (26 October 2026); Calgary allows no refund or deferment.

Can I take the TCF in Lethbridge?

No TCF was offered on the University of Lethbridge's pages on 26 October 2026: its French Language Centre runs the DELF-DALF twice a year. The nearest TCF Canada centre is Calgary.

Where are TCF Canada seats available?

On 26 October 2026: in New Brunswick (CCNB in Edmundston and Bathurst, every weekend to 20 December; Alliance française de Moncton from 1 December, $390) and in Ottawa (10 November, $400).
