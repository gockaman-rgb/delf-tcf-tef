TCF Canada test centres in Canada: the 47 approved centres, province by province

By Augusto Grone · Updated 8 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

In Canada, you can take the TCF Canada and the TCF Québec at one of 47 test centres approved by France Éducation international (48 entries on its list of 19 September 2026, one centre appearing twice) — Alliances Françaises, universities, CEGEPs and training centres — in nine provinces and Nunavut, 44 of them with computer-based sessions. Below: the list by province and city, with the contact details FEI publishes, and the registration rules we found at the main centres.

Key facts
  • 47 approved centres (48 FEI entries, with Gaspé listed twice), list of 19 September 2026: 23 in Quebec, including 7 in Montreal, 7 in Ontario, 5 in British Columbia, 3 in Alberta.
  • 45 entries with computer-based sessions; paper-based only in Sherbrooke, in Lethbridge and at the University of Victoria.
  • Each centre sets its own price, often unpublished: $390 (Alliance française, Vancouver), $400 (Alliance française, Edmonton), $440 (UQTR); nothing posted at the Alliance Française de Montréal.
  • ⚠️ Sessions in Toronto and Vancouver fill up within minutes: register the moment registration opens, not as your test date approaches.
  • 20 days between two attempts, whichever centre you test at; results in 2 to 4 weeks.
  • No centre in Prince Edward Island, Yukon or the Northwest Territories.

How to register for the TCF Canada in Canada

FEI’s list doesn’t say which versions a centre runs (Canada, Québec, tout public, IRN), or its dates and fees: you check those on the centre’s website or by phone — our city guides below did it for the main centres.

In Canada, each centre handles its own registrations online, on its own website, with the passport you’ll use for your IRCC application. Registration is final, and cancelling costs money: the Alliance Française de Montréal keeps $75 if you cancel more than 15 days ahead, and refunds nothing after that. In the big cities, sessions fill up within minutes: create your account in advance and log in the moment registration opens. Allow at least 20 days between two attempts, whichever centre you test at. Results arrive by email within 2 to 4 weeks and are valid for two years. On test day, bring a valid passport and your printed test-day notice (admission letter).

City guides: Montreal, Toronto, Quebec City, Ottawa, Vancouver

For the largest cities, one page brings together the approved TCF centres with their contacts, what their websites showed on 17 September 2026 — versions, prices, dates — and how to register:

  • TCF Canada in Montreal

    Seven approved centres, all computer-based, plus Laval, Kirkland and Saint-Constant; fees rarely published.

  • TCF Canada in Toronto

    Four Alliance française campuses and GB Language Centre; quarterly sessions, registration at 10 am a month before.

  • TCF Canada in Quebec City

    Collège Stanislas and Université Laval, both computer-based, for the TCF Canada and the TCF Québec.

  • TCF Canada in Ottawa

    Alliance française Ottawa (computer or paper) and FrancoLangues in Kanata; no entry without a passport and a printed notice.

  • TCF Canada in Vancouver

    $390 at the Alliance française Canada Pacific, where every listed session was sold out on 17 September 2026.

The 47 approved centres, province by province

How to read this list. It reproduces the official list of test centres approved by France Éducation international (FEI), read on 19 September 2026: name, address, phone, generic email and website as FEI publishes them; where the listed website no longer works or has moved, we give the one we opened that day. “Computer-based”: FEI lists the centre as offering computer sessions; “paper-based”: FEI lists no computer sessions. An organization that is not on this list is not approved.

One addition since: read again on 8 October 2026, FEI’s list includes one centre that is not on the 19 September list used below — Collège Boréal in Windsor, Ontario (7515 Forest Glade Drive; paper-based, according to FEI).

Frequently asked questions

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Montreal?

At one of the seven approved centres in Montreal — Alliance Française de Montréal, Collège Stanislas, UQAM, Concordia University, Cégep Marie-Victorin, Centre Yves-Thériault, Collège ELC — or in the suburbs, in Laval, Kirkland and Saint-Constant. All of them offer computer-based sessions.

Where can I take the TCF Canada in Toronto?

At the Alliance française de Toronto (Spadina, North York, Mississauga and Oakville campuses) or at GB Language Centre in North York. The Alliance’s sessions are quarterly; registration opens a month before, at 10 am, and fills up within minutes.

How much does the TCF Canada cost in Canada?

There is no national fee, and many centres publish nothing: $390 at the Alliance française in Vancouver, $400 in Edmonton, $440 at UQTR among the centres we checked; the Alliance Française de Montréal posts no price.

Do all these centres offer both the TCF Canada and the TCF Québec?

Most offer both, but FEI’s list doesn’t say: check which test you tick when you register — the TCF Québec, which is modular, is not accepted by IRCC.

What do I need to bring on test day?

A valid passport and your printed test-day notice — no exceptions in Ottawa; your identity is checked throughout the test. For computer-based tests, the centre provides a QWERTY keyboard.
