DELF exam dates 2027 in Poland: sessions, fees in złoty and the 15 centres

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

Lire en français

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Poland (terminy egzaminów DELF 2027) were not yet published on 26 October 2026. The next session runs from 16 to 20 November 2026, with the DELF B2 on 18 November; the Alliances françaises take registrations until 6 November, while at the Institut français in Warsaw they closed on 18 October. The DELF and DALF are held at 15 centres on the same dates; the B2 costs 620 zł (527 zł at the reduced rate).

Key facts
  • 15 test centres (FEI list of 26 October 2026), under the central management of the Institut français de Pologne: the Institut in Warsaw and Kraków, eight Alliances françaises, the Lycée français de Varsovie, Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań and three associations.
  • November 2026: A1 and A2 on the 16th, B1 on the 17th, B2 on the 18th, C1 on the 19th, C2 on 20 November; speaking tests from 13 to 25 November at the Alliances.
  • Registration: until 6 November 2026 at the Alliances françaises; 21 September to 18 October at the Institut français in Warsaw (closed).
  • In 2026, three tout public sessions: February (B2 on the 4th), June (B2 on the 24th) and November; 2027 dates not published.
  • 2026 fees: A1 300 zł · A2 380 zł · B1 520 zł · B2 620 zł · C1 750 zł · C2 800 zł, 15% less at the reduced rate.
  • Results about one month after the session; diploma about three months after (Alliances françaises).

Registration

At the Institut français, you register online on the session page, in Warsaw or Kraków; at the Alliances françaises — Gdańsk, Katowice, Lublin, Łódź, Rzeszów, Toruń, Wrocław —, with your chosen Alliance’s form. A certificate of achievement must be requested in writing when you register. The diplomas are independent: you register directly for the level you want, and you need 50 points out of 100 to pass.

The 15 DELF-DALF test centres, city by city

Here the DELF and DALF are run by a central management body, which FEI’s list shows separately from the centres: Gestion Centrale DELF/DALF (Warsaw) · ul. Widok 12, 00-023 Varsovie · institutfrancais.pl. It sets the national session calendar; candidates register with a centre.

Frequently asked questions

What are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Poland?

Not yet published: on 26 October 2026, neither the Institut français nor the Alliances françaises had released their 2027 calendar. In 2026, the tout public sessions were in February, June and November.

When is the next DELF exam in Poland?

From 16 to 20 November 2026: A1 and A2 on the 16th, B1 on the 17th, B2 on the 18th, DALF C1 on the 19th and C2 on 20 November. The Alliances françaises take registrations until 6 November; at the Institut français in Warsaw, registration closed on 18 October.

How much does the DELF B2 cost in Poland?

620 zł, or 527 zł at the reduced rate, at the Institut français and at the Alliances françaises alike (2026 fees). The DALF C1 costs 750 zł and C2 800 zł.

Where can I take the DELF in Warsaw?

At the Institut français de Pologne, which runs the November session (online registration, closed on 18 October 2026), or at the Lycée français de Varsovie, also on FEI’s list.
