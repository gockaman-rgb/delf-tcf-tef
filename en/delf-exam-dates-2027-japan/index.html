DELF exam dates 2027 in Japan: sessions, registration and fees

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 4 min read

The DELF exam dates 2027 in Japan were not yet published on 26 October 2026. The last 2026 adult session is on Saturday 7 and Sunday 8 November (registration closed on 3 October). You register with one of Japan’s ten Instituts français and Alliances françaises, at the desk or in their online shop, during the fixed windows listed by the national centre on delfdalf.jp; most centres hold two sessions a year, in spring and autumn.

Key facts
  • 2027: dates not yet published (checked on 26 October 2026); they will appear on delfdalf.jp.
  • Autumn 2026: Saturday 7 November (A1, B1, C1) and Sunday 8 November (A2, B2, C2).
  • 2026 adult sessions: 21 March (A1 to B2), 13–14 June and 7–8 November; junior on 17 May and 11 October.
  • Fees: A1 ¥12,500 · A2 ¥15,500 · B1 ¥20,500 · B2 ¥24,500 · C1 ¥29,500 · C2 ¥31,500.
  • 10 centres, from Sapporo to Naha; registration at the centre or in the Instituts français’ online shops.
  • Results online at app.resultats.delfdalf.jp, with your 12-digit candidate number and date of birth.

The 2026 sessions, and 2027

Japan has a national calendar, published by the Centre national DELF/DALF Japon: the written papers are on the same weekend in every centre, the speaking tests may fall on other days depending on the centre. The 2027 dates had not been published on 26 October 2026.

How to register

You register with the centre, not with the national centre: at its reception desk, or in the Instituts français’ online shops, during the published windows, which cannot be changed. You give your name, date and place of birth, nationality, e-mail and phone number — and, if you have already taken a DELF or DALF, the candidate number you were given then. A parent or guardian signs the form for candidates under 18. Calendar and forms: delfdalf.jp.

Adults, juniors and children

The DELF and DALF tout public are for adults and older teenagers, with no upper age limit. The DELF junior is for 12- to 18-year-olds (spring 2026: Tokyo, Yokohama, Osaka, Nagoya, Sendai) and the DELF Prim for 8- to 12-year-olds (Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya). All three lead to the same diploma.

Results and diplomas

Results are published only online, on app.resultats.delfdalf.jp: enter the 12-digit candidate number on your notice to attend, without hyphens, and your date of birth. Nothing is given by phone or e-mail. You pass with 50/100 or more; below 5 points in any of the four skills (10 for the C2) you are eliminated, as you are if you miss a paper. A candidate who failed may ask in writing, within seven days of the results, to see their paper — without re-marking. If you need proof before the diploma arrives, a certificate can be sent to you.

Preparing while you wait for the dates

The exam itself does not change from one country to another: in Japan as in France, the DELF A1 to B2 and the DALF C1 have four sections scored out of 25, you pass with 50/100, and a score below 5/25 in any one section fails you; the diploma is issued by the French Ministry of Education and is valid for life. France Éducation international publishes free official sample papers — DELF B2, DELF B1, DALF C1 — with the answer keys, the listening recordings and the marking grids. Frequently asked questions

When are the DELF exam dates 2027 in Japan?

Not yet published on 26 October 2026. In 2026, the adult sessions were on 21 March (A1 to B2), 13–14 June and 7–8 November; the 2027 calendar will appear on delfdalf.jp.

When is the next DELF exam in Japan?

Saturday 7 November 2026 (A1, B1, C1) and Sunday 8 November 2026 (A2, B2, C2); registration closed on 3 October 2026.

How much is the DELF B2 in Japan?

¥24,500 (delfdalf.jp, 26 October 2026). The other levels: A1 ¥12,500, A2 ¥15,500, B1 ¥20,500, DALF C1 ¥29,500 and C2 ¥31,500, with a reduction for students of the Instituts français and Alliances françaises.

Can adults take the DELF in Japan?

Yes: the DELF and DALF tout public are for adults and older teenagers, with no upper age limit; the DELF junior (12 to 18) and the DELF Prim are for younger candidates.

And the TCF in Japan?

TCF dates are set by each centre. The national centre lists the TCF Canada at the Instituts français in Kyoto and Osaka and at the Alliance française in Nagoya, at ¥42,000, and the TCF tout public at ¥20,000 on paper or ¥23,000 on computer (26 October 2026).

How do I book the DELF in Japan?

At your centre’s reception desk or in the Instituts français’ online shops, during the fixed registration window of each session — for autumn 2026, 28 August to 3 October. Centres close registration when they are full.
