TCF Canada in Halifax: the Alliance Française, the fee and the sessions

By Augusto Grone · Updated 26 October 2026 · 5 min read

In Halifax, the TCF Canada is held by the Alliance Française Halifax, 5509 Young Street — the only approved TCF centre in Nova Scotia — for $400, on computer. On 26 October 2026, its booking page listed a single session, Tuesday 8 December, already full. You register online with a scan of your passport, the fee is not refundable, and the certificate arrives by email 15 business days after the test. The nearest open seats were in New Brunswick.

Booking the TCF Canada in Halifax

The Alliance sells the TCF Canada in its online store, on a registration page where each session shows its date, the venue and «Full» or a booking button: on 26 October 2026, the only session listed, Tuesday 8 December at 9 am, was full. To book a future date you upload a scanned copy of your passport, pay — online, by phone with a Visa or Mastercard, or by Interac e-transfer — and receive your official notice 7 to 10 days before the test. Dates are firm: a change is considered only for exceptional circumstances, with documents, at least 7 days before, and a candidate turned away without a passport must wait a month to reapply. Questions go to exam@afhalifax.ca or 902-580-8284.

On 26 October, the nearest open dates were in New Brunswick: $390, every weekend to 20 December at the CCNB in Edmundston and Bathurst, and from 1 December in Moncton.
